### Key features
- RON current account + Visa debit card + Smart Mobile app included
- Free instant payments and RON incoming transfers up to 49,999 lei
- Free RON cash withdrawals at ATMs of ANY bank in Romania
- Free RON savings account (Super Acces Plus, 0 lei admin fee)
- Smart Hour: RON<->EUR exchange at BNR rate Mon-Fri 10:00-11:00, max 1,500 EUR/day, 10,000 EUR/month
- 'Plătești și economisești' automatic round-up style saving on card payments
- Overdraft interest discount of 5 percentage points vs standard (standard margin 9.99% -> 4.99% over IRCC)
- Smart Market cashback/vouchers; Google Pay / Apple Pay / Garmin Pay
- Welcome bonus up to 600 lei for new customers (01.09.2026-31.01.2027, see regulation)

### Fees
- **Monthly package administration fee:** 0 lei if conditions met, else 15 lei/month - Zero when BOTH: (1) at least one 'electronic transaction' in the month (POS/online card payment, Smart Mobile payment, Direct Debit or FixPay) AND (2) monthly credit turnover of at least 1,000 lei. Customers aged 18-24 pay 0 even without a payment. Source: fee list 1 Sep 2026 + product page.
- **Account opening/closing:** 0 lei - Fee list s.1.1
- **Card delivery fee:** 15 lei - Debited from the attached account for newly issued/reissued/renewed debit cards
- **Smart Mobile subscription:** 0 lei in ZERO Simplu/ZERO Tot - Standard price 4 lei/month
- **Interbank RON transfer in Smart Mobile:** 0 lei in ZERO Simplu/ZERO Tot - Standard 4 lei + clearing fee 0.51 lei (<50,000 lei)
- **Instant payments / RoPay (RON):** 0 lei - Fee list s.2.1; note the Smart Mobile page shows 0.45 lei for the standard (non-ZERO) price
- **Cash withdrawal at ATMs in Romania (RON):** 0 lei - Included in package
- **Gambling card transactions:** 1% + 10 lei - Fee list s.6

### Eligibility
- Individual (persoană fizică) aged 18+
- New customers: Romanian citizen, 18+, can open fully online with ID photo + short selfie video (about 8 minutes)
- Existing customers can switch to the package from Smart Mobile or branch
- Zero fee needs: >=1,000 lei monthly credits + 1 electronic payment/month

## What it is
ZERO Simplu is Raiffeisen's basic current-account package for individuals. It bundles a RON current account, a Visa debit card (the "Cardul Galben" family) and the Smart Mobile app, and is the account on which most of the bank's digital features (Smart Hour FX, Plătești și economisești, instant payments) are offered at no extra cost.

## Zero-fee conditions (quoted)
"Trebuie doar să îndeplinești cumulativ următoarele două condiții: să faci lunar cel puțin o plată, cât de mică ... cu cardul, telefonul sau ceasul – la comercianți sau online; din aplicația de mobile banking Smart Mobile; prin Direct Debit sau FixPay. și să ai încasări lunare în contul curent deschis de minim 1.000 lei. Altfel, plătești 15 lei lunar aferenți comisionului de administrare pachet și te bucuri de aceleași beneficii."

- "Dacă ai între 18 și 24 de ani, nu plătești nimic chiar dacă nu faci nicio plată pe lună."
- Fee list footnote: ZERO Simplu fee is 0 lei if monthly credit turnover >= 1,000 lei AND one electronic transaction per month. NOT counted as electronic transactions: incoming transfers, transfers between own accounts (except via FixPay), cash withdrawals, automatic debits by the bank for fees/loan instalments.
- Turnover (rulaj) does not count transfers between the holder's own accounts or credited loan proceeds (per fee-list definition for comparable packages).

## What is included at zero cost (if the condition is met)
- RON current account + debit card; Smart Mobile; instant payments and incoming RON transfers up to 49,999 lei; free RON cash withdrawals from any bank's ATM in Romania; a RON savings account (Super Acces Plus) with no admin fee; Smart Market; Smart Hour FX (Mon-Fri 10:00-11:00, BNR rate, max 1,500 EUR/day and 10,000 EUR/month); Plătești și economisești; Direct Debit utility payments with 0 commission (ConfortaBill).
- Overdraft: access to a preferential overdraft rate (see 'overdraft-descoperit-de-cont').

## Opening
- 100% online in Smart Mobile: download app, confirm contact data, photograph ID, record a short video, pick the ZERO Simplu package, sign electronically; card is sent home. Open to Romanian citizens aged 18+ who are not yet customers.
- Alternatively leave your data on the website for a call-back or visit a branch.

## Promotions (time-limited)

- "Adu-ți prietenii": up to an extra 2,000 lei when bringing friends (same period).
