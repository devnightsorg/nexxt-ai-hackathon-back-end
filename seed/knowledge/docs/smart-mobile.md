# Raiffeisen Smart Mobile (mobile banking)

## At a glance (structured data)
- **Slug:** smart-mobile
- **Category:** digital_service
- **Summary:** Raiffeisen's mobile banking app: open accounts/deposits/loans, instant payments up to 49,999 lei 24/7, RoPay (send money by phone number), Smart Hour FX, savings and investment tools, card control and push notifications.
- **Interest rate / return:** none or not applicable for this product (see details; any rate not found is stated as NOT FOUND)
- **Minimum amount:** not specified / see details
- **Currencies:** RON, EUR, USD
- **Good for:** daily_spending, saving_tools, money_management

### Key features
- Open account, savings, deposits, Flexicredit (about 10 minutes), investment plans and insurance from the app
- Instant Payments 24/7/365 up to 49,999 lei per transaction; RoPay: send/receive money using a phone number
- Free push notifications for transactions; card block/unblock; PIN view
- Smart Hour FX, Smart Market cashback, Plătești și economisești (SavingBox)
- See total interest earned on savings and deposits (helps decide whether to file the annual single declaration)
- Google Pay / Apple Pay / Garmin Pay enrolment

### Fees
- **Monthly subscription:** 0 lei in ZERO Simplu / ZERO Tot; 4 lei standard
- **Instant payments:** 0 lei in ZERO packages (RoPay 0 lei per fee list); standard price on the product page 0.45 lei - Pages disagree for non-ZERO customers
- **Intrabank own accounts:** 0 lei
- **Intrabank between customers:** 3 lei (0 in ZERO)
- **Interbank RON:** 4 lei + 0.51 lei clearing (<50,000 lei) or 6 lei (>=50,000 lei); 0 in ZERO
- **External RON payments:** 4 lei
- **FX payments interbank:** 4 lei + SWIFT 50 lei/message

### Eligibility
- Individuals with at least a current account or a Raiffeisen credit card
- Non-customers: onboarding in app with ID photo + selfie video, about 8 minutes
