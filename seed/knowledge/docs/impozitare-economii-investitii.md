# Taxation highlights for savings, deposits, funds and pensions

Only items stated on Raiffeisen pages are quoted; items from third-party sources are flagged.

- **Interest on deposits and savings accounts**: "Plata dobânzii se efectuează după reținerea impozitului de 10% pe venituri din dobânzi, conform prevederilor legale în vigoare." (Raiffeisen deposit and savings FAQs, retrieved 2026-10-28.) The bank withholds the tax; Smart Mobile shows the interest received so the customer can check whether the annual single declaration is needed.
- **Health insurance contribution (CASS) on interest** - third-party guide (financer.ro via search, not Raiffeisen): 10% CASS may apply only when net non-wage income including interest exceeds a threshold of 6 minimum gross salaries; the first-half-2026 threshold was cited as 24,300 lei (minimum wage 4,050 lei) and recalculated from July 2026. UNVERIFIED - confirm with ANAF before relying on it.
- **Investment funds (RAM)**: "câștigurile ... aferente răscumpărărilor realizate vor fi impozitate la sursă"; SAI RAM calculates, withholds and pays the tax on each redemption with a gain, so the investor does not file the single declaration for these gains. The page distinguishes units held more than or less than 365 days but states only 'conform codului fiscal în vigoare' - the percentage is NOT given on the page.
- **Voluntary pension (Pilon III)**: contributions are tax-deductible up to the RON equivalent of 400 EUR/year; amounts paid out are subject to legal deductions applicable at payment date.
- **Government securities (Fidelis/Tezaur-type)**: one third-party source says they are fully tax-exempt - not covered by Raiffeisen pages; UNVERIFIED.
- **Dividends** (third-party): tax rose from 10% to 16% for dividends distributed from 1 January 2026 - relevant only to direct equity holdings, not to RAM fund redemptions.
- Securities-trading customers: Declarația 200 due by 25 May for certain income; Declarația 201 for foreign-source securities/dividends (MiFID page).
- The bank does not provide tax advice for financial-instrument transactions (MiFID document).
