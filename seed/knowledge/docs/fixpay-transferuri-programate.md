# FixPay - ordine de plată programate / transferuri automate

## At a glance (structured data)
- **Slug:** fixpay-transferuri-programate
- **Category:** digital_service
- **Summary:** Scheduled payment orders: set a recurring transfer (e.g. salary account -> savings account, Flexidepozit, SmartInvest style transfers) that executes automatically; free for transfers to Raiffeisen savings and Flexidepozit; counts as an 'electronic transaction' for package fee waivers.
- **Interest rate / return:** none or not applicable for this product (see details; any rate not found is stated as NOT FOUND)
- **Minimum amount:** not specified / see details
- **Currencies:** RON
- **Good for:** regular_saving, habit_building, bills_automation

### Key features
- Automatic recurring transfers between your accounts (e.g. monthly saving)
- Free service for automatic transfers into Super Acces Plus and Flexidepozit
- FixPay payments count as an electronic transaction for the monthly zero-fee condition of packages

### Fees
- **FixPay scheduled transfer:** 1.5 lei/transfer (fee list) - Pages state FixPay is free for transfers to savings accounts and Flexidepozit
- **Direct debit:** 1.5 lei/payment (fee list); 0 for utilities in ZERO Simplu via ConfortaBill

### Eligibility
- Raiffeisen current account holder

"Poți economisi regulat prin setarea unui transfer lunar automat din contul tău curent sau din contul de salariu în contul de economii, prin serviciul FixPay" and "serviciul FixPay gratuit" for Flexidepozit. Fee list s.2.2: Ordine de plată programată - FixPay 1.5 lei/transfer; debit direct 1.5 lei/payment. Direct Debit utility payments are advertised as zero commission in ZERO Simplu (service ConfortaBill).
