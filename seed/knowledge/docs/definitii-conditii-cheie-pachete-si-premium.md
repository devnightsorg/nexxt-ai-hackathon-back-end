# Key definitions: electronic transaction, monthly turnover, Premium customer, client de casă

## 'Tranzacție electronică' (fee list footnote)
Any payment made at POS or online with the debit card or credit card included in the package, via **Smart Mobile**, via **Debitare Directă (Direct Debit)** or **FixPay**.
NOT an electronic transaction: any incoming interbank/intrabank payment (including to the credit-card account); transfers between the holder's own current/card accounts at the bank (except those made through FixPay); cash withdrawals; automatic debits by the bank for fees/commissions and loan instalments.

## Monthly credit turnover ('rulaj creditor') / 'încasări lunare'
Sum credited in the month to the holder's current accounts. For comparable packages the fee list excludes sums credited through intra-bank transfers between the holder's own accounts and sums from loans granted by the bank. Incoming = cash deposits at Raiffeisen MFMs plus interbank/intrabank transfers (e.g. salary), excluding transfers between own accounts (deposit-offer footnote).

## Package zero-fee conditions (fee list 1 Sep 2026)
- ZERO Simplu: >= 1,000 lei monthly credit turnover AND one electronic transaction/month, else 15 lei (18-24 year-olds pay 0).
- Gold: >= 2,000 lei monthly credit turnover AND one electronic transaction, else 30 lei.
- ZERO Tot: one electronic transaction per month (from an account in the package or via the included credit card), else 20 lei.
- Premium: monthly electronic transaction AND (total balance of holder's accounts >= 40,000 EUR OR average monthly income >= 2,000 EUR equivalent), else 60 lei. (Package page: average monthly inflows of 2,000 EUR over the last 3 months or 40,000 EUR under Raiffeisen Group management in the last calendar month.)
- Diaspora: balance >= 5,000 lei (current + term deposit + savings accounts at month end, BNR rates) OR monthly credit turnover >= 1,000 lei, else 20 lei.

## 'Client Premium' (loan pricing)
"Clientul Raiffeisen Premium: venit lunar de minimum 2.000 EUR (echivalent lei) încasat în cont Raiffeisen în ultimele 3 luni sau clientul care are active financiare în administrarea Grupului Raiffeisen de minimum 40.000 EUR, pachet Premium." Used for lowest mortgage/refinancing/Flexicredit prices (Flexicredit Premium discount 1.2%).

## 'Client de casă' (Flexicredit pricing)
Customer who receives recurring monthly income of at least 2,000 RON in a Raiffeisen account (1% interest reduction). Income calculation excludes: transfers between own Raiffeisen accounts, loans granted by Raiffeisen, cancelled transactions, interest from Raiffeisen savings products.

## FWR / Elite segment
Customers holding an FWR package with end-of-previous-month balance >= 250,000 EUR equivalent - excluded from the 'bonus deposits'.

## Minimum net wage criteria
Loan pages require income 'cel puțin egal cu venitul minim net pe economie' (the exact RON value is not stated on the pages).
