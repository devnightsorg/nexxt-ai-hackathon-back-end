# SmartInvest - plan de investiții recurente în fondurile Raiffeisen

## At a glance (structured data)
- **Slug:** smartinvest-plan-investitii-recurente
- **Category:** digital_service
- **Summary:** Monthly automatic investment plan from your current account into any Raiffeisen Asset Management fund from 200 RON / 50 EUR / 50 USD per month; zero cost to open and close; stop any time.
- **Interest rate / return:** none or not applicable for this product (see details; any rate not found is stated as NOT FOUND)
- **Minimum amount:** 200 RON (or 50 EUR / 50 USD per month)
- **Currencies:** RON, EUR, USD
- **Terms:** Flexible, no fixed term
- **Good for:** long_term_growth, regular_saving, retirement, child_education, home_purchase

### Key features
- Automatic monthly debit from the current account into chosen fund(s) (since July 2024 all 13 RAM funds are eligible)
- Minimum 200 RON / 50 EUR / 50 USD per month (depending on fund currency)
- 0 cost at opening and closing the plan; modify amounts or stop whenever you want
- If the monthly amount is below one unit price, start with a purchase of at least one unit
- View portfolio in real time in Smart Mobile
- Cancel in Smart Mobile via 'Plăți viitoare' or sign a revocation form in branch

### Fees
- **Plan opening / closing:** 0
- **Fund ongoing costs:** 0.86% - 2.43% per year depending on fund - See each fund

### Eligibility
- Appropriateness test / MiFID questionnaire completed
- Raiffeisen current account
- Open from Smart Mobile: Produse -> Fonduri de investiții

## Quoted facts

Raiffeisen Asset Management S.A., prin transferul automat din contul tău curent." "Pornind de la o sumă lunară minimă de 200 RON/50 EUR/50 USD." Zero costs at opening/closing.
Fund ongoing costs listed: RON FLEXI 0.99%, RON Plus 1.29%, Conservator RON 1.73%, Moderat RON 1.90%, România Dividend 2.43%, EURO FLEXI 0.86%, EURO Plus 0.95%, EURO Obligațiuni 1.08%, Conservator Euro 1.53%, Moderat Euro 1.70%, Sustainable Mix 1.89%, Sustainable Equity 2.32%, DOLAR Plus 1.32%.
Recommended holding: bond funds 1-5 years; funds with an equity component 2-10 years (but redemption allowed any time).
Risk warnings (quoted in essence): investments are not deposits and not FGDB-guaranteed; past performance is no guarantee; possible loss of the whole investment; local funds are not covered by an investor-compensation fund for market losses; FX effects for non-RON funds.
Tax: redemption gains taxed at source by SAI RAM (rate not stated on the page).
