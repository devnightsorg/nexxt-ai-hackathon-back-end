# Asigurarea completă a locuinței Welcome Home (UNIQA)

## At a glance (structured data)
- **Slug:** asigurare-locuinta-welcome-home
- **Category:** insurance
- **Summary:** Voluntary comprehensive home insurance with cover up to 3,000,000 lei, price fixed for 5 years, PAD (mandatory natural-disaster policy) handled by the bank, issued 100% in Smart Mobile in about 5 minutes; claims paid within 7 working days of complete documents.
- **Interest rate / return:** none or not applicable for this product (see details; any rate not found is stated as NOT FOUND)
- **Minimum amount:** not specified / see details
- **Currencies:** RON
- **Terms:** Policy with price fixed 5 years
- **Good for:** home_protection, home_purchase

### Key features
- Covers home and contents up to 3,000,000 lei (about 600,000 EUR): storms, floods, earthquake, avalanche, accidental leaks, installation damage (boilers, A/C, solar panels), pest-control interventions
- Price fixed for 5 years if conditions unchanged; 10% discount with green-home certificate
- Bank arranges the mandatory PAD policy if you do not have one
- Temporary accommodation up to 250 RON/day (max 5 days); rent cover 2,000 RON/month up to 3 months (limit 6,000 RON); 24/7 plumber/electric intervention up to 1,000 RON/year; civil liability up to 25,000 RON/year; emergency measures up to 15% of the sum insured
- Sum insured chosen by the customer based on real home value; 10% of it automatically covers contents
- Claims 100% digital (daunerapide.uniqa.ro, phone 0374.400.400), paid in max 7 working days after all documents

### Fees
- **Premium:** Not published on the page (personalised in Smart Mobile)
- **PAD (mandatory):** Arranged by the bank if missing - Mortgage examples use PAD = 130 lei

### Eligibility
- Raiffeisen customer with Smart Mobile; new customers can open an account in 8 minutes first
- Insured home owned by customer or parents/grandparents (needs an active PAD policy)
- Insurer: UNIQA Asigurări S.A.
