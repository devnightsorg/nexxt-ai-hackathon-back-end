# Cont de economii «Plătești și economisești» (SavingBox)

## At a glance (structured data)
- **Slug:** plateste-si-economisesti
- **Category:** savings_account
- **Summary:** Automatic saving on card payments: choose 1%, 3%, 5% or 10% of every debit-card payment to be moved into a dedicated RON savings account paying 10% p.a. on the first 5,000 lei (variable), 5% up to 50,000 lei and 2.5% above.
- **Interest rate / return:** 10.0 % p.a. (RON, on first 5,000 lei; variable) (as of 2026-10-28). Bands: <=5,000 lei 10.00%; 5,001-50,000 lei 5.00%; >50,000 lei 2.50%. Rate is variable. Account can be opened only together with activation of the 'Plătești și economisești' function and funded only by it. Interest accrues daily, credited monthly; 10% tax at source (per savings-account FAQ).
- **Minimum amount:** not specified / see details
- **Currencies:** RON
- **Terms:** No fixed term
- **Good for:** short_term_saving, emergency_fund, habit_building, round_up_saving

### Key features
- Activate in Smart Mobile at no cost and pick 1%, 3%, 5% or 10% of each debit-card payment to set aside automatically
- Dedicated RON savings account paid 10.00% up to 5,000 lei, 5.00% for 5,001-50,000 lei, 2.50% above 50,000 lei (variable, marginal bands)
- Account can only be funded by the feature's automatic transfers
- 0 lei monthly administration fee; instant access to the money
- Included at no cost with ZERO Simplu / Gold / Premium

### Fees
- **Monthly administration:** 0 lei - RON savings account dedicated to the service
- **Card cash withdrawal from the account:** 1.5%, min 10 lei - Standard debit-card cash fee depending on package

### Eligibility
- Customer with a RON debit card/current account and Smart Mobile
- Feature available for payments made with the debit card in RON

## What it is
A "round-up style" automatic saving feature: "Activezi opțiunea din Smart Mobile și alegi procentul din fiecare tranzacție cu cardul pe care vrei să îl pui deoparte. Banii ajung în contul tău de economii la care ai super dobândă!" The Smart Mobile page calls the activation "SavingBox" and lists percentages of 1%, 3%, 5% or 10% of every debit card payment from the RON account.

Footnotes: "Contul de economii Plătești și Economisești poate fi deschis exclusiv împreună cu activarea funcționalității de economisire la plata cu cardul de debit în lei" and "Alimentarea contului ... este permisă exclusiv din tranzacții generate de funcționalitatea 'Plătești și Economisești'". Rate is variable and tiered per slice, interest calculated daily and capitalised monthly; new sums earn interest from the day of the transaction.

## Practical notes
- Because the account can be funded only by the automatic round-ups, the 10% band (first 5,000 lei) fills slowly: e.g. (inferred arithmetic) 5% of 2,000 lei monthly card spending = 100 lei/month.
- Included at zero cost in ZERO Simplu, Gold, Premium.
- For larger lump sums use Super Acces Plus or term deposits.
- The 10% headline is a variable promotional-type rate - the bank can change it; always show 'as_of' date.
