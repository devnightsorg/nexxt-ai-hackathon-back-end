## Raiffeisen Moderat RON - KID (quoted, 17.01.2025)
- ISIN ROFDIN0002N6. Objective: "grad de risc mediu și un potențial de creștere moderat". Same fund-of-funds policy as Conservator RON, more equity-tilted allocation allowed; actively managed, no benchmark.
- SRI: the KID text states "clasa de risc 2 din 7" (and the aggregator also lists 2/7) although the investor profile is 'risc mediu (moderat)' - flag this inconsistency; verify the current KID before presenting the SRI.
- Recommended holding 3 years; costs: management & administrative 1.82%/year + transaction 0.08%/year (= 1.90% on SmartInvest page); no entry/exit fee at publication; no performance fee.
- KID scenarios for 10,000 lei over 3 years: moderate 11,071 lei (+3.45%/yr); unfavourable 10,303; stress 8,437; favourable 12,018.
