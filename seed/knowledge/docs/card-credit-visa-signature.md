# Card de credit Raiffeisen Visa Signature

## At a glance (structured data)
- **Slug:** card-credit-visa-signature
- **Category:** credit_card
- **Summary:** High-end credit card (limit 15,000-120,000 lei, net income at least 2,000 EUR) with 10 free LoungeKey entries, preferential FX abroad, 0.5% cashback in Romania, 0% ATM cash fee worldwide and 22% interest; annual fee 500 lei.
- **Interest rate / return:** 22.0 % p.a. (revolving/cash balance) (as of 2026-10-28). DAE example 51.85% (5,367 lei over 12 months; high because the 500-lei annual fee is included).
- **Minimum amount:** 15000 RON
- **Currencies:** RON
- **Good for:** travel, premium_lifestyle, lounge_access

### Key features
- Limit 15,000 - 120,000 lei; daily cash limit 10,000 lei
- 10 free LoungeKey airport lounge entries per year for holder + guest, then 30 EUR/entry
- Preferential FX rate abroad; 3/6 interest-free instalments for purchases above 300 lei abroad; zero ATM cash fee worldwide (page)
- 0.5% cashback on RON purchases at supermarkets, fuel stations, pharmacies, restaurants, cafes in Romania (not for instalment-posted purchases)
- Free travel insurance; up to 4 free supplementary cards
- Annual fee 500 lei; interest 22% on revolving balance (DAE example 51.85%)

### Fees
- **Annual account administration fee (any number of cards):** 500 lei - Due at first debit/credit movement and yearly after
- **Issue / reissue / renewal fee:** 25 lei - Reissue not charged if at bank's initiative or damaged card
- **Merchant payments:** 0%
- **Cash withdrawal at Raiffeisen ATM:** 0%
- **Balance enquiry at Raiffeisen ATM:** 1 leu (free in Smart Mobile)
- **Penalty interest rate (rata dobânzii penalizatoare):** 3% p.a. per fee list - Fee list s.7; how it combines with the normal rate is defined in the contract (not retrieved)
- **Optional 'Protecția rambursărilor' insurance:** 0.5% of the highest credit used in the cycle (+ unpaid instalment part) - After a free period
- **LoungeKey:** 10 free entries/year then 30 EUR/person
- **Cash withdrawal other-bank ATMs:** Fee list shows 2% for the premium column group; product page says 0% at ATMs worldwide - conflict, verify

### Eligibility
- Age 22-65
- Net income at least 2,000 EUR per applicant
- At least 3 months at current workplace
- No executed loans / delays
