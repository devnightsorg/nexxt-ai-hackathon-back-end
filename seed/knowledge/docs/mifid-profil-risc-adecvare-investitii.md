# Investment suitability, MiFID questionnaire and fund risk rules

## What the bank states (quoted / paraphrased from the MiFID II presentation document, 26.10.2026, and the MiFID page)
- Raiffeisen Bank is authorised by ASF (Decision A75/30.01.2014 etc.) for investment services under Legea 126/2018 / Reg. ASF 5/2019, including investment advice as an authorised activity.
- **But for its retail customers the bank provides investment services WITHOUT advice**: "RBRO furnizează pentru Clienții săi servicii de investiții financiare fără consultanță." Under this model the bank applies the **appropriateness test (test de oportunitate)**: it collects information on the client's knowledge and experience for each type of instrument, "astfel încât Banca să fie în măsură să evalueze dacă serviciile de investiții sau instrumentele financiare avute în vedere sunt oportune". Purpose: "asigurarea înțelegerii de către Client a implicațiilor și nivelului de risc asociat investițiilor".
- For **retail clients** (the default category for all individuals) the bank evaluates (i) knowledge and experience via the appropriateness test and, where relevant, (ii) whether the investment is adequate to the financial situation and objectives (suitability).
- If the client gives too little or inaccurate information the bank may be unable to assess appropriateness; if the bank concludes the product is not appropriate it **warns the client**, who "ar trebui să apeleze la consultanță externă"; if the client still proceeds after a clear warning, the client assumes the risks.
- The bank recommends clients provide complete, accurate and up-to-date information on knowledge, experience, financial situation, risk tolerance and capacity to bear losses.
- Execution-only (no test) is offered only to institutional eligible counterparties, not to individuals.
- Clients are told their category (retail / professional / eligible counterparty) before any service; reclassification applies to all transactions.
- **Investor compensation**: Raiffeisen Bank is a founding member of the Fondul de Compensare a Investitorilor (FCI); compensation ceiling = RON equivalent of **EUR 20,000 per investor** for failure of the investment-services provider (not for market losses).
- Conflicts of interest and incentives (stimulente) are disclosed in the MiFID document; the bank does not accept incentives for order execution; fund distribution costs are disclosed in the 'Document de informare cu privire la costuri'.
- Documents to read before investing (per the SmartInvest page): fund prospectus and DIE/KID on raiffeisenfonduri.ro, the bank's MiFID presentation document, terms of the unit-intermediation contract, order-execution policy, cost-information document.
- Contact for investor-compliance questions: conformitate_servicii_financiare@raiffeisen.ro.

## Fund risk indicator (SRI) scale used by RAM funds
SRI runs 1 (lowest risk) to 7 (highest) and "arată nivelul de risc ... în comparație cu alte produse". The RAM funds sit at 2-4: RON Plus/Flexi, Euro Plus/Flexi, Dolar Plus, Conservator and Moderat funds at 2 (per KID/aggregator); Euro Obligațiuni and Sustainable Mix at 3; România Dividend and Sustainable Equity at 4 (aggregator). SRI does not capture liquidity, counterparty and derivatives risks (KID).
Recommended holding periods: bond funds 1-5 years; funds with equity component 2-10 years (SmartInvest FAQ).

## Agent operating rules (INFERRED guidance for the hackathon agent - not Raiffeisen text)
1. Before recommending any investment fund, SmartInvest plan, unit-linked policy or pension fund, check that the user has a completed risk-profile / MiFID appropriateness questionnaire in the system; if not, say that the bank will ask the questionnaire at subscription and do NOT frame the output as personalised investment advice - present it as information about products that match the stated goal and horizon.
2. Only suggest funds whose recommended holding period is <= the user's stated horizon; never suggest equity funds (SRI >= 4, holding 7-10 years) for horizons under 5 years or for an emergency fund.
3. Keep an emergency buffer (the bank's own guidance: 3-6 months' salary, 'fond de urgență de minimum 3-6 salarii') in instant-access savings before investing.
4. Always add: not a deposit, not FGDB-guaranteed, capital can be lost, past performance not a guarantee, costs apply (show the annual cost %).
5. Don't quote returns that were not retrieved; fund performance data was not available to this knowledge base.
