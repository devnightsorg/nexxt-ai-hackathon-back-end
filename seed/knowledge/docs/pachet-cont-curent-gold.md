# Pachet de cont curent Gold

## At a glance (structured data)
- **Slug:** pachet-cont-curent-gold
- **Category:** account_package
- **Summary:** Mid-tier package: RON account with Visa Gold debit card, an extra EUR or USD current account, 3 free airport business-lounge entries per year, BlackCab discount and all ZERO Simplu benefits; 0 lei/month if you receive 2,000 lei/month and make one card payment.
- **Interest rate / return:** none or not applicable for this product (see details; any rate not found is stated as NOT FOUND)
- **Minimum amount:** not specified / see details

- **Good for:** daily_spending, travel, salary_account

### Key features
- Visa Gold debit card + RON current account
- Additional current account in EUR or USD
- 3 free Business Lounge entries per year (Romania and Vienna airports) for payments of minimum 2,000 lei
- 12% discount at BlackCab rides; road-assistance / home concierge discounts
- All ZERO Simplu benefits (Smart Mobile, instant payments, free RON ATM cash-out, Smart Hour, Plătești și economisești)
- Preferential-interest term deposits (3 and 12 months) for salary customers
- Welcome bonus up to 600 lei for new customers (01.09.2026-31.01.2027)

### Fees
- **Monthly package administration fee:** 0 lei if conditions met, else 30 lei/month - Zero when BOTH: >=1 electronic payment per month AND monthly credit turnover >= 2,000 lei.
- **Card delivery fee:** 15 lei
- **Stand-alone Visa Gold debit card (not in a package):** Issue 70 lei; annual 200 lei; min. initial deposit 300 lei - Fee list s.6; these do not apply when the card is inside the package
- **Account in other currency:** Included in package - Outside a package: 3 EUR/month admin for FX current accounts

### Eligibility
- Individual aged 18+
- Zero fee needs >=2,000 lei monthly credits + 1 electronic payment/month
- Opens online in Smart Mobile (about 8 minutes) for new customers

## What it is
Gold sits between ZERO Simplu and Premium. It adds a Visa Gold debit card, a second current account in EUR or USD and travel perks.

## Zero-fee conditions (quoted)
"Trebuie doar să îndeplinești cumulativ următoarele două condiții: să faci lunar cel puțin o plată ... și să ai încasări lunare în contul curent deschis de minim 2.000 Lei. Altfel, plătești 30 lei lunar aferent comisionului de administrare pachet."

## Included benefits
- Visa Gold debit card (RON) and RON current account; EUR or USD current account.
- 3 free Business Lounge entries/year in selected airports in Romania and Vienna, for payments of at least 2,000 lei (details in the lounge regulation).
- "Călătorești cu BlackCab": 12% discount on rides in several Romanian cities when paying with the Visa Debit Gold card.
- Discounts on annual road-assistance subscriptions and home "concierge" services (Visa offers).
- Zero commission for RON cash withdrawals from any bank ATM in Romania (when the card is part of a current-account package).
- Smart Hour FX (BNR rate, Mon-Fri 10-11, max 1,500 EUR/day, 10,000 EUR/month).
- Preferential deposit interest for salary customers on 3- and 12-month RON/EUR deposits ("bonus de dobândă") - see 'depozit-bonus-dobanda-incasari'. The Gold page states a bonus of 0.50 pp (EUR) and 0.3 pp (RON) over standard; the rate table shows +0.30 pp for RON 3m/12m and +0.20/+0.40 pp for EUR 3m/12m - the rate table is treated as authoritative.

## Opening
Online via Smart Mobile (about 8 minutes) for new customers, or in branch. Campaign: up to 600 lei guaranteed bonus 01.09.2026-31.01.2027.
