# KYC / AML basics at Raiffeisen Bank (cunoașterea clientelei)

## Legal basis (quoted)
"Actualizarea datelor personale reprezintă o procedură obligatorie prevăzută de lege ... reglementată prin Legea Nr. 129/2019 și prin Regulamentul Nr. 2/2019 împotriva spălării banilor și a finanțării terorismului." (Raiffeisen page 'Actualizarea datelor'; also cites BNR Regulation 9/2008.)

## What customers must do
- Provide identification documents and, on request, information on the **purpose and economic rationale of transactions, the source and destination of funds**, tax residency and tax identification number (CGB 3.1.3). The bank may ask for proof of the **source of funds invested in virtual currencies** and the financial path of such funds.
- **Periodically confirm/update identification data.** Customers who do not update "în termenul prevăzut de lege" risk **temporary blocking of access to accounts**. Update options: nearest branch, or in **Smart Mobile** (button available after renewing a Romanian ID card).
- New online customers: photograph the ID card + record a short video selfie (automatic video verification) or choose the 'no ID card' path with an identification call. The bank cooperates with DGEP (Direcția Generală pentru Evidența Persoanelor) for identity checks.

## What the bank may do (CGB 3.1.4)
Refuse a product or a payment, limit transactions (e.g. cash, card, mobile banking), block account/card/app, terminate contracts or the entire relationship, when: information is missing or inconsistent; there are reasonable suspicions of money laundering, terrorist financing, fraud or sanctions violations (including signals from other banks/authorities); authorities impose restrictions; the client is a designated/sanctioned party; transactions involve unregistered virtual-currency exchanges or payment providers from EU-designated high-risk or non-cooperative jurisdictions; or the client/transaction does not fit the bank's risk profile.

## Definitions in CGB
Beneficial owner (beneficiar real) = any natural person who ultimately owns or controls the client and/or on whose behalf a transaction is carried out. Politically exposed persons (persoane expuse public) have a dedicated definition (functions and a period after which the status ends).

## Other reporting
FATCA (US tax) and CRS (OECD) - the bank identifies relevant clients and reports to ANAF. Sanctions lists (EU, UN, US OFAC, UK OFSI) are applied (CGB definitions).

## Deposit-guarantee interplay
Deposits whose holder identity was not verified before they became unavailable are excluded from FGDB compensation - another reason to keep KYC data current.
