# Fondul de Garantare a Depozitelor Bancare (FGDB) - 100,000 EUR coverage

## Summary (quoted from the 'Formular pentru informațiile oferite deponenților', Raiffeisen Bank)
- Deposits at Raiffeisen Bank are protected by the **Fondul de Garantare a Depozitelor Bancare (FGDB)**, the statutory scheme under Legea 311/2015.
- **Coverage ceiling: the RON equivalent of EUR 100,000 per depositor per bank** ("per deponent per bancă"). All deposits of the same person at the same bank are added together: "dacă un deponent deține un cont de economii în valoare de 90.000 EUR și un cont curent în care are 20.000 EUR, acesta va primi doar echivalentul în lei al sumei de 100.000 EUR."
- **Joint accounts**: the 100,000 EUR ceiling applies separately to each holder.
- **Payout deadline**: compensation is made available within **7 working days** from the date the deposits become unavailable; currency of payment: **RON**. If entitlement is uncertain or the deposit is under litigation, the deadline is extended.
- **Compensation formula**: guaranteed deposits + accrued interest - instalments, commissions and other debts owed to the bank that are due at the time deposits become unavailable. Limited to the ceiling.
- **Prescription**: the right to receive compensation expires 5 years after FGDB makes it available (longer if the bankruptcy procedure of the bank exceeds 5 years).
- **Temporary higher coverage (12 months)**: deposits arising from (a) real-estate transactions for residential property; (b) retirement, redundancy, marriage, divorce, invalidity or death of the depositor; (c) insurance indemnities or compensation for crime damages / wrongful convictions. Natural persons must inform the bank (form + documents) when they receive such sums. The ceiling for these cases is set and periodically reviewed by BNR.
- **Contacts**: FGDB - Str. Negru Vodă nr. 3, București 030774, tel. 021.326.60.20, comunicare@fgdb.ro, www.fgdb.ro. Raiffeisen Bank - Calea Floreasca 246 D, tel. +4021.306.1000.

## What is covered at Raiffeisen (per product pages)
Current accounts, savings accounts (Super Acces Plus, Plătești și economisești), term deposits (incl. Fresh Money, Flexidepozit), card accounts, nominal deposit certificates. Product FAQs: "Disponibilitățile din conturile de depozit la termen sunt garantate în România prin FGDB în limitele și cu excepțiile prevăzute în Legea nr. 311/2015."

## Excluded deposits (quoted list, abridged)
Deposits of credit institutions in own name; own-funds instruments; deposits from transactions with a final conviction for money laundering; deposits of financial institutions, investment firms, insurers/reinsurers, collective investment undertakings, pension funds, central/local/regional public authorities; deposits whose holder identity was not verified (AML) before they became unavailable; debt securities issued by the bank and own acceptances/promissory notes.

## NOT covered by FGDB (important for recommendations; FGDB covers deposits only - the list below is the bank's statement for funds and inference for the rest)
Investment fund units (Raiffeisen Asset Management funds), voluntary pension fund assets, unit-linked and savings life insurance, government securities held in custody - "investițiile în fondurile de investiții nu sunt depozite bancare, nefiind garantate de FGDB" (SmartInvest page). Investor losses from market movements are not compensated by any scheme; the Fondul de Compensare a Investitorilor (see 'mifid-profil-risc-adecvare-investitii') only covers failure of the intermediary, up to the RON equivalent of 20,000 EUR per investor.

## Agent rules (inferred, not bank text)
- When a user's combined balances (current + savings + deposits + card) at Raiffeisen exceed the RON equivalent of 100,000 EUR, mention that the excess is not guaranteed and that spreading across banks or non-deposit instruments changes the protection.
- Never describe fund units, pension fund or unit-linked policy assets as 'guaranteed by FGDB'.
