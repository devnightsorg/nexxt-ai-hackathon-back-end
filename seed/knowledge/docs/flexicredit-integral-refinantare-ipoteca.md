# Flexicredit Integral - refinanțare garantată cu ipotecă

## At a glance (structured data)
- **Slug:** flexicredit-integral-refinantare-ipoteca
- **Category:** mortgage
- **Summary:** Mortgage-backed refinancing loan to merge several loans into one: 5,000-200,000 EUR equivalent, 6 months-30 years, up to 85% of the pledged property value; fixed 3 years from 4.85% or 5 years from 5.45% (Premium, green property), then IRCC + 2.10%.
- **Interest rate / return:** 4.85 % p.a. fixed 3 years (then variable IRCC + 2.10%) (as of 2026-10-28). Premium customers refinancing exclusively mortgage loans on a property with energy certificate A/B (after 15.02.2023) or RoGBC and taking life insurance. Standard offer 6.50% fixed 3y then IRCC + 3.75% = 9.32%. Cost sheet 22.10.2026.
- **Minimum amount:** 5000 EUR (equivalent in RON)
- **Currencies:** RON
- **Terms:** 6 months - 30 years
- **Good for:** debt_consolidation, lower_monthly_payments

### Key features
- Merge all existing loans into one with a lower fixed instalment; fixed rate for 3 or 5 years
- Premium + life insurance pricing: 3y 4.85% / 5y 5.45%, then IRCC + 2.10% (7.67%); standard 3y 6.50%, then IRCC + 3.75% (9.32%)
- Amount 5,000 - 200,000 EUR equivalent; term 6 months - 30 years; up to 85% of pledged property value (depends on location)
- Collateral: mortgage on one or more properties of borrower, co-debtor and/or first-degree relatives/in-laws
- Page advertises a 2,000 lei bonus when refinancing at Raiffeisen (regulation dated 2025-07-08 - check current validity)

### Fees
- **Analysis fee:** 900 lei
- **Valuation fee:** 605 lei apartment / 787 lei house (VAT incl.)
- **PAD insurance:** 130 lei
- **Conditional payment order (for refinancing):** Applies - amount not retrieved (cost sheet lists 'Comision ordin plată condiționat') - Fee list: conditional payment orders 5 EUR issuance (SWIFT included) when used to take over loans from other banks
- **Early repayment commission:** See cost sheet (not parsed reliably)

### Eligibility
- Age 21 to 63 (women) / 65 (men) at final maturity - applies to applicant and co-debtors with income
- Income at least the minimum net wage per economy for each participant
- At least 3 months at current workplace (permanent; fixed-term covering the loan; or fixed-term with >=2 successive renewals)
- No executed loans / loans repaid with major repeated delays
- Documents: ID, ANAF income consent (applicant + co-debtor), property documents (antecontract, ownership, land-book extract, valuation documents)
- Meeting criteria does not give a right to the loan; bank may refuse without detailed reasons

## Quoted terms
- "Credit de refinanțare cu ipotecă, acum cu dobândă fixă 3 ani sau 5 ani ... îți aduni toate creditele într-unul singur, mai avantajos, cu o rată lunară fixă mai mică și dobândă redusă."
- Valoare: între 5.000 și 200.000 Euro (echivalent Lei); perioada: minimum 6 luni, maximum 30 de ani; valoarea maximă finanțată: până la 85% din valoarea imobilelor aduse în garanție (depends on location).
- Rates: 3 years "de la 4,85%, ulterior variabilă: marjă 2.10% + IRCC"; 5 years "de la 5,45% ... marjă 2.10% + IRCC". Cost sheet: Premium 4.85% / standard 6.50%; variable IRCC+2.10% (7.67%) vs IRCC+3.75% (9.32%); DAE 7.79% (Premium fixed 3y) / 9.08% (standard).
- Example: 350,900 lei, 25 years, fixed 3y 4.85% then 7.67%, for Premium clients refinancing exclusively mortgage loans, where the original property has an energy certificate A/B issued after 15.02.2023 or RoGBC.
- Bonus: "primești și BONUS 2.000 lei în cont atunci când îți refinanțezi creditele la noi" (regulation 08.07.2025) - validity beyond 2025 not confirmed.
