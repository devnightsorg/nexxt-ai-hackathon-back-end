# Card de credit / cumpărături Raiffeisen Visa Standard

## At a glance (structured data)
- **Slug:** card-credit-visa-standard
- **Category:** credit_card
- **Summary:** Entry credit card in RON (limit 700-50,000 lei) with up to 56 days interest-free grace, annual fee 40 lei, 26% interest on revolving balance, interest-free instalments 3/6/9 months anywhere via Smart Mobile and up to 12 fixed instalments at Multishop partners.
- **Interest rate / return:** 26.0 % p.a. (interest on revolving/cash balance; 0% on merchant purchases repaid in full by due date) (as of 2026-10-28). Fee list 1 Sep 2026; DAE example: 5,367 lei repaid in 12 equal instalments at 26% -> instalment 512.71 lei, total 6,152.52 lei, DAE 32.40%.
- **Minimum amount:** 700 RON
- **Currencies:** RON
- **Terms:** Credit line of undetermined period; instalments 3-60 months
- **Good for:** shopping, short_term_liquidity, instalment_purchases

### Key features
- Limit 700 - 50,000 lei; merchant limit 100% of credit line, cash 40% (daily cash max 3,000 lei)
- Grace period up to 56 days: 0% interest on merchant purchases if the full amount is repaid by the due date
- Minimum monthly repayment 5% of used amount (min 10 lei)
- Interest 26% p.a. on revolving balance/cash/transfers (DAE example 32.40%); penalty 3%
- Split purchases in 3 / 6 / 9 interest-free instalments anywhere in the world from Smart Mobile (campaign 01.07 - 31.10.2026): 3 for any amount, 6 for >=500 lei, 9 for >=1,000 lei; up to 12 fixed interest-free instalments at Multishop partners; reduced-interest instalments 14.99%-19.99%
- Up to 4 free supplementary cards; Apple Pay / Google Pay / Garmin Pay
- Yellow Friday promo: 150 lei bonus for transactions >= 1,000 lei when the card is requested via Smart Mobile (campaign 20.10 - 02.11.2026)

### Fees
- **Annual account administration fee (any number of cards):** 40 lei - Due at first debit/credit movement and yearly after
- **Issue / reissue / renewal fee:** 25 lei - Reissue not charged if at bank's initiative or damaged card
- **Merchant payments:** 0%
- **Cash withdrawal at Raiffeisen ATM:** 0%
- **Balance enquiry at Raiffeisen ATM:** 1 leu (free in Smart Mobile)
- **Penalty interest rate (rata dobânzii penalizatoare):** 3% p.a. per fee list - Fee list s.7; how it combines with the normal rate is defined in the contract (not retrieved)
- **Optional 'Protecția rambursărilor' insurance:** 0.5% of the highest credit used in the cycle (+ unpaid instalment part) - After a free period
- **Cash withdrawal at other Romanian banks / abroad ATM:** 1% of amount + 2.5 lei - Fee list s.7 (Standard/eMAG/Platinum column group as parsed)
- **Reduced-interest instalments ('Rate cu dobândă redusă'):** 3/6 months 19.99%; 12/18 months 17.99%; 24/36/48/60 months 14.99%

### Eligibility
- Age 22-65
- Net family income at least the minimum net wage per economy
- At least 3 months at current workplace
- No executed loans / no loans repaid with delays
- Employees and pensioners can apply with ID only (no income certificate) - per page

## Key terms (fee list 1 Sep 2026 + product page)
- Max credit limit 50,000 lei (Standard and eMAG); daily cash limit 3,000 lei; cash max 40% of the limit per month; grace period up to 56 days; min repayment 5% (min 10 lei) plus any instalment installments and excess over limit.
- Annual administration fee 40 lei (Standard); issue fee 25 lei.
- Interest on revolving balance 26% (Standard, eMAG; the Platinum example also uses 26%); Signature/Infinite 22%.
- Interest-free instalments in Smart Mobile "Împarte în rate": 3 months any amount, 6 months >= 500 lei, 9 months >= 1,000 lei - campaign 01.07.2026 - 31.10.2026 (ends in 3 days from retrieval date). "Până la 12 rate fixe fără dobândă" at Multishop partners (>800 partners).
- "Prin apel la *2000 poți cere ca o sumă de pe cardul de cumpărături să fie transferată în contul tău curent" in fixed instalments.

## How to get it
Existing customers: digital card in Smart Mobile, physical card by post. Non-customers: apply online or in branch. Documents: ID (employees/pensioners without income certificate per page); other documents may be requested.

## Warnings
Credit card cost is high on revolving balances (DAE 32.40% in the bank's own example). Agent should recommend only if the user will repay in full within the grace period or uses 0% instalments, and should not suggest it to users already showing overdue payments or high debt service.
