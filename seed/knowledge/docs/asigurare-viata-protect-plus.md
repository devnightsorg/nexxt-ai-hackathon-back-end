# Asigurări de viață cu economisire garantată - Junior Protect Plus / Senior Protect Plus (UNIQA)

## At a glance (structured data)
- **Slug:** asigurare-viata-protect-plus
- **Category:** insurance
- **Summary:** Endowment-type life insurance with guaranteed savings: from 100 lei/month, policy issued on the spot, no medical exam. Junior Protect Plus builds capital for a child; Senior Protect Plus gives protection and savings for the adult. Guaranteed insured sum plus a guaranteed supplementary benefit at maturity.
- **Interest rate / return:** not published / not retrieved guaranteed (not retrieved) (as of 2026-10-28). The guaranteed interest rate / supplementary benefit is stated in the KID (Junior/Senior Protect Plus KID dated 2026-06-19) - figures not retrieved.
- **Minimum amount:** 100 RON (per month)
- **Currencies:** RON
- **Terms:** Multiple durations (not retrieved)
- **Good for:** child_education, retirement, long_term_saving, protection

### Key features
- Minimum premium 100 lei/month; 4 monthly-premium variants; Junior: flexible contract durations; Senior: 3 durations
- Pays the insured sum + supplementary benefit (guaranteed) at maturity, plus a share of the insurer's investment result if any
- Junior: if the policyholder dies from an accident, UNIQA pays the remaining premiums and the child still receives the sum at maturity
- Senior: accident-related invalidity/death protection; UNIQA takes over premiums on invalidity
- Premium paid automatically by ConfortaBill direct debit (free for insurance contract holders)
- No medical exam or occupation assessment; grace period for unpaid premium, then suspension; surrender value may apply

### Fees
- **Monthly premium:** from 100 lei/month - 4 premium variants

### Eligibility
- Sold in any Raiffeisen branch (contract signed with the relationship manager)
- Junior: for parents/relatives wanting to save for a child
- Insurer: UNIQA Asigurări de Viață S.A.
