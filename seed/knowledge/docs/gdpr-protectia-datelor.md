# Data privacy (GDPR) summary - Raiffeisen Bank customers

## Controller and contacts (quoted)
Raiffeisen Bank S.A. is the data controller. Data Protection Officer: **dpo@raiffeisen.ro**; general contact centrala@raiffeisen.ro, call centre *2000. The privacy policy has been applicable since 25.05.2018 under Regulation (EU) 2016/679.

## Purposes of processing (CGB 2.1 / privacy policy, abridged)
Provision of banking services and contract performance; identity verification (incl. ID data); **KYC / AML / sanctions**; fraud prevention (e.g. the 'Serviciul de Afișare Nume Beneficiar' payee-name check with TRANSFOND); credit risk assessment and reporting to **Biroul de Credit** and **Centrala Riscului de Credit** (BNR); regulatory reporting (FATCA/CRS to ANAF); insurance and investment-services distribution (appropriateness / suitability assessments); complaints handling; marketing (with the right to opt out); recording of phone calls and video monitoring; website cookies; chatbot on raiffeisen.ro; data collected in online banking apps.

## Recipients
Group entities (Raiffeisen Group in Romania incl. SAI Raiffeisen Asset Management, Raiffeisen Broker de Asigurare-Reasigurare, Raiffeisen Leasing, Aedificium Banca pentru Locuințe, and Raiffeisen Bank International AG), contractual partners and processors, insurers for which the bank acts as intermediary, BNR / CRC, ASF, ANAF, DGEP, courts and authorities. Transfers outside Romania go to EU/EEA states (e.g. Austria, Slovakia, Greece, Hungary, Germany, Ireland) and, with safeguards such as EU standard contractual clauses, to third countries; a copy of the safeguards can be requested.

## Credit Bureau retention (privacy policy 5.8.2)
Data recorded in the Biroul de Credit system are stored and disclosed to participants for up to **4 years** from the update date; data of applicants who withdrew or were refused are kept for a shorter period (not retrieved).

## Customer rights (quoted list)
Information; access; rectification; erasure ("dreptul de a fi uitat"); restriction; **data portability**; **objection** (always for direct marketing); the right not to be subject to a decision based solely on automated processing, incl. profiling, with human review on request; the right to complain to **ANSPDCP** (B-dul Gen. Gheorghe Magheru 28-30, sector 1, București, tel. 318.059.211, anspdcp@dataprotection.ro) or to court.
Response deadline for data-protection complaints: **one month, extendable up to 3 months** in exceptional cases (bank complaints procedure sheet).

## Agent guidance (inferred)
- The agent should only use the customer's transaction data for the purpose of the recommendation session and should say what data it used. Offer to explain which products are suggested and why; the customer may object to profiling for marketing.
- Do not share personal data with third parties; for data-subject requests point to dpo@raiffeisen.ro.
