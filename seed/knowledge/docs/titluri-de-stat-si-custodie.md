# Titluri cu venit fix (government securities) and custody service - Premium/Private Banking only

## Fixed-income securities (quoted)
- Offer: treasury certificates, government bonds and municipal bonds; currencies RON, EUR, USD; minimum investment = value of one security; can be sold before maturity on the secondary market (price may be above or below purchase price). "Îți oferim garanția randamentului atât timp cât titlurile sunt deținute până la scadență."
- **Eligibility: "clienți Raiffeisen Private Banking sau Premium cu active în administrare >= 60,000 EUR echivalent în Grupul Raiffeisen România."**
- Raiffeisen acts as intermediary and primary dealer for the Ministry of Finance issues. Contact: tel. 021.306.1991, treasury.sales@raiffeisen.ro, Mon-Fri 9-17.
- "Titlurile de stat sunt investițiile cu cel mai scăzut grad de risc, iar statul emitent îți garantează investiția" - state-issued, not FGDB (which covers deposits).
- No yields, fees or maturities are published on the page -> NOT FOUND.

## Custody of fixed-income instruments
Account for financial instruments, settlement (buy/sell/transfer) on local and foreign markets, record-keeping, reporting/statements, portfolio valuation, issuer relations (coupon and principal collection). Fees are negotiated by portfolio value and can be renegotiated. Contact custody@raiffeisen.ro / treasury.sales@raiffeisen.ro / 021 306 19 91.

## Agent guidance (inferred)
Only relevant for Premium/Private customers with >= 60,000 EUR; otherwise suggest term deposits or low-risk RAM funds.
