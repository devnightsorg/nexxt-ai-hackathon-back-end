# Asigurări de viață unit-linked - Future Invest Protect / Active Invest Protect (UNIQA)

## At a glance (structured data)
- **Slug:** asigurare-viata-invest-protect
- **Category:** insurance
- **Summary:** Unit-linked life insurance (50% protection / 50% investment according to the bank's FAQ) for medium/high income customers: Future Invest Protect for a child, Active Invest Protect for an adult. Value linked to chosen investment funds; sums and premiums indexed yearly; free GenePlanet DNA test at 250 lei/month premium.
- **Interest rate / return:** none or not applicable for this product (see details; any rate not found is stated as NOT FOUND)
- **Minimum amount:** not specified / see details
- **Currencies:** RON
- **Terms:** Medium/long term (not retrieved)
- **Good for:** child_education, long_term_growth, protection

### Key features
- Protection against death/invalidity plus investment in funds chosen by the customer according to risk profile
- FAQ: 50% of the money goes to protection, 50% to investment
- Sums insured and premium adjusted annually for inflation
- Free GenePlanet DNA test if minimum monthly premium is 250 lei and at least two payments are made
- Not capital-guaranteed: policy value depends on fund performance

### Fees
- **Minimum monthly premium for the GenePlanet gift:** 250 lei - Minimum premium for the product itself not retrieved

### Eligibility
- For customers with medium or high income interested in protection plus medium/long-term investing
- Meeting with a Raiffeisen officer in branch / call-back form
- Insurer: UNIQA Asigurări de Viață S.A.

Distribution: Raiffeisen Bank S.A. acts as affiliated agent (cod RAJ-500196, ASF) for UNIQA; insurance is optional unless stated; before signing the customer must read the pre-contractual information (IPID/KID), conditions and the bank's 'Intermediere asigurări' notice.
