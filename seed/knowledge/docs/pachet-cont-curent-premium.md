# Pachet de cont curent Premium

## At a glance (structured data)
- **Slug:** pachet-cont-curent-premium

- **Summary:** Top retail package for Premium customers: Visa Platinum debit + Platinum credit card, multi-currency accounts, 6 lounge entries, accident insurance, travel insurance and preferential credit/deposit pricing. Zero monthly fee with 2,000 EUR average monthly income or 40,000 EUR held with Raiffeisen.
- **Interest rate / return:** none or not applicable for this product (see details; any rate not found is stated as NOT FOUND)

### Key features
- Visa Platinum debit card + RON current account + extra RON account
- EUR and USD current accounts; EUR or USD debit card; payments and receipts in the account currency
- Visa Platinum credit card included
- 6 free Business Lounge entries per year (Romania + Vienna)
- 0% interest on the first 500 lei used from overdraft
- Accident insurance (death or total permanent disability from accident) + travel insurance on Platinum cards
- Visa merchant discounts; all ZERO Simplu benefits
- Premium status unlocks preferential mortgage/consumer-loan rates and bonus deposits

### Fees
- **Monthly package administration fee:** 0 lei if conditions met, else 60 lei/month - Zero when: monthly electronic transaction AND (average monthly income >= 2,000 EUR equivalent over last 3 months OR >= 40,000 EUR equivalent total balance with Raiffeisen Group in the last calendar month) - per fee list footnote: total balance of holder's accounts >= 40,000 EUR + 1 electronic transaction, OR average monthly income >= 2,000 EUR + 1 electronic transaction.
- **Card delivery fee:** 15 lei
- **Stand-alone Visa Platinum debit (outside package):** Issue 70 lei; annual 200 lei; min deposit 300 lei - Fee list s.6 shares the Gold/Platinum column

### Eligibility
- Premium customer profile: average monthly incoming income >= 2,000 EUR equivalent (last 3 months) OR >= 40,000 EUR equivalent held with Raiffeisen Group
- Make at least one electronic payment per month for the fee waiver

## What it is
The Premium package is the bank's "Premium Banking" account bundle. The conditions below are quoted from the package page and the fee list (1 Sep 2026).

## Included
- Visa Platinum debit card and current account; additional RON current account; EUR and USD accounts; EUR or USD debit card; payments and receipts in the currency of the included account.
- Visa Platinum credit card included in the package.
- 6 free entries per year in Business Lounges at Romanian airports and Vienna.
- "0% dobândă pentru primii 500 de Lei utilizați din descoperirea de cont (overdraft)".
- Insurance package: death from accident / total permanent disability from accident; travel insurance attached to the Platinum debit and credit cards.
- All ZERO Simplu benefits.

## Zero-fee condition (quoted from page)
"... să faci lunar cel puțin o plată ... și să ai 2.000 EUR, echivalent în Lei, încasări medii lunare în ultimele 3 luni sau 40.000 EUR, echivalent în Lei, în administrarea Raiffeisen Grup România (în ultima lună calendaristică). Altfel, plătești 60 lei lunar."

## Why "Premium" matters for other products
Raiffeisen mortgages (Casa Ta, Casa Ta Verde, refinancing) and Flexicredit show their lowest rates for "clienți Premium", defined as: monthly income >= 2,000 EUR received in a Raiffeisen account in the last 3 months OR financial assets >= 40,000 EUR under Raiffeisen Group management, with the Premium package.
