# Flexicredit Refinanțare - credit de refinanțare fără ipotecă

## At a glance (structured data)
- **Slug:** flexicredit-refinantare-fara-ipoteca
- **Category:** personal_loan
- **Summary:** Unsecured RON loan to refinance consumer loans/cards from other banks into one fixed instalment, from 5.55% fixed (campaign 15.10-26.11.2026), 500-50,000 EUR equivalent, with optional extra cash and a 3-month payment holiday after 6 months.
- **Interest rate / return:** 5.55 % p.a. fixed (minimum of range 5.55% - 17.95%) (as of 2026-10-28). Same pricing logic as Flexicredit: lowest rate with >=2,000 RON recurring income at Raiffeisen and optional insurance; DAE 7.88% - 39.4%.
- **Minimum amount:** 500 EUR (equivalent in RON)
- **Currencies:** RON
- **Terms:** 18 months - 10 years (see conditions)
- **Good for:** debt_consolidation, lower_monthly_payments

### Key features
- Refinance any loans from other banks into a single fixed monthly instalment
- Fixed rate 5.55% - 17.95% (DAE 7.88% - 39.4%)
- Amount 500 - 50,000 EUR equivalent in RON; optional additional sum (extra cash) repayable over max 5 years
- Term 18 months - 10 years (10 years only when refinancing loans taken before 08.07.2020; 5 years for loans granted after that date)
- 3 months pause on instalments available after the first 6 months; optional life & unemployment insurance (2% rate discount)

### Fees
- **Analysis fee:** 200 lei - Example
- **Monthly administration fee:** 5 lei - Example
- **Early repayment commission:** Not retrieved for this product page; Flexicredit standard cost sheet shows 0.50% (last year) / 1.00% (before last year) - Verify in the product's full cost sheet

### Eligibility
- Age 21 to 63 (women) / 65 (men) at loan end
- Income >= minimum net wage
- >=3 months at current workplace
- Documents: ID of applicant (and spouse if applicable) + ANAF income consent; extra documents possible

- "Perioada maximă: 10 ani pentru creditele refinanțate acordate înainte de data de 08.07.2020. 5 ani pentru creditele refinanțate acordate după data de 08.07.2020."
- "Rata de dobândă fixă este cuprinsă între 5.55% și 17.95%. Valoarea minimă DAE este de 7.88% iar valoarea maximă DAE este de 39.4%."
- Campaign "De Yellow Friday ... refinanțezi orice credite cu dobândă fixă de la 5,55%", valid 15.10 - 26.11.2026.
- Benefits: one fixed instalment, extra amount option, "3 luni pauză la plata ratelor ... după primele 6 luni de rambursare", optional life & unemployment insurance.

## Examples (quoted)
50,000 lei over 10 years at 5.55% (incl. 200 analysis fee + 7,200 lei single insurance premium): instalment 548.87 lei, DAE 9.81%, total 65,864.52 lei. 18 months at 5.55%: 2,906.42 lei, DAE 9.52%. 60 months at 10%: 1,067.35 lei, DAE 14.57%. 17.95% over 60 months: 1,273.31 lei, DAE 19.94%.
