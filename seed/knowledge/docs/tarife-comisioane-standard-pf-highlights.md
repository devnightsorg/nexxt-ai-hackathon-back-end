## 7. Credit cards (Standard / eMAG / Platinum / Signature / Infinite)
- Max limit: 50,000 (Standard & eMAG) / 90,000 (Platinum) / 120,000 (Signature) / 320,000 lei (Infinite). Daily cash: 3,000 / 5,000 / 10,000 / 20,000 lei (column grouping as parsed). Monthly cash max 40% of the limit. Grace up to 56 days. Minimum repayment 5% (min 10 lei).
- Annual account fee: 40 / 60 / 150 / 500 / 2,000 lei. Issue/renew 25 lei. ATM balance enquiry 1 leu.
- Merchant payments 0%; Raiffeisen ATM cash 0%; other Romanian bank / foreign ATM: 1% + 2.5 lei (Standard-group) or 2% (premium group as parsed).
- Interest: 0% on merchant purchases repaid in full by due date; 26% on revolving balance (Standard/eMAG/Platinum per product pages) and 22% (Signature/Infinite); penalty rate 3%; reduced-interest instalments 19.99% (3/6 months), 17.99% (12/18), 14.99% (24-60).
- Optional insurance 'Protecția rambursărilor' 0.5% of highest limit used; protection package 1.5 lei/month only for old Standard/eMAG cards; travel insurance free for Platinum/Signature/Infinite (older optional policy 50 lei/year for policies bought before 11 July 2024).
