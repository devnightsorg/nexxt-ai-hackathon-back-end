# Flexicredit - credit de nevoi personale fără ipotecă

## At a glance (structured data)
- **Slug:** flexicredit-nevoi-personale
- **Category:** personal_loan
- **Summary:** Unsecured RON consumer loan from 500 to 50,000 EUR equivalent over 18-60 months, fixed rate 5.55%-17.95%, approved on the spot and available 100% online in Smart Mobile; optional life & unemployment insurance gives a 2% rate discount.
- **Interest rate / return:** 5.55 % p.a. fixed (minimum of range; range 5.55% - 17.95%) (as of 2026-10-28). Minimum rate valid for customers receiving >= 2,000 RON recurring monthly income in a Raiffeisen account and adding the optional life+unemployment insurance (Uniqa). Cost sheet 15.10.2026: 'client de casă' 5.55%-14.95%; Premium 5.35%-14.75% (with insurance). DAE range 7.90%-39.4%.
- **Minimum amount:** 500 EUR (equivalent in RON)
- **Currencies:** RON
- **Terms:** 18 months - 60 months
- **Good for:** debt_consolidation, car_purchase, home_renovation, unexpected_expenses, education, wedding_event

### Key features
- Fixed rate for the whole loan: 5.55% - 17.95% p.a. (DAE 7.90% - 39.4%); campaign 'dobândă fixă de la 5,55%' valid 15.10 - 26.11.2026
- Amount: 500 - 50,000 EUR equivalent in RON; term 18 months - 5 years; no collateral
- Online in Smart Mobile in about 10 minutes if eligible; ID card + consent to consult ANAF income database is usually all you need
- Rate depends on profile: 'client de casă' (>=2,000 RON recurring monthly income in a Raiffeisen account) -1%; Premium -1.2%; optional life+unemployment insurance -2%
- 3 months payment holiday from the 7th month of repayment on request (max 3 instalments)
- Free change of the instalment due date; equal or decreasing instalments

### Fees
- **Analysis fee (comision analiză dosar):** 200 lei - From representative examples
- **Monthly administration fee:** 5 lei - From representative examples
- **Early repayment commission:** 0.50% in the last year of the contract; 1.00% before the last year - Cost sheet 15.10.2026 'Comision de plată anticipată'
- **Optional life & unemployment insurance (Uniqa):** Single premium financed in the loan = loan value x 0.12% x number of loan months - Examples: 3,600 lei on 50,000 lei over 60 months; 1,080 lei over 18 months; 7,200 lei over 120 months (refinancing)
- **Payment holiday:** 3 instalments from month 7 - On request; interest continues per contract (conditions not retrieved)

### Eligibility
- Age 21 to 63 (women) / 65 (men) at the end of the loan
- Income at least equal to the minimum net wage in the economy
- At least 3 months at current workplace (permanent contract, or fixed-term covering the loan, or fixed-term with >=2 successive renewals)
- No executed loans or loans repaid with large repeated delays
- Meeting all criteria does not guarantee approval; bank may refuse without detailed reasons

## Discounts (cost sheet 15.10.2026)
"oferta clienți de casă: 1% reducere la dobândă pentru încasarea venitului recurent lunar de minim 2.000 RON în cont Raiffeisen; client Premium: 1.2% reducere la dobândă"; insurance discount 2%. Cost sheet ranges: client de casă 5.55%-14.95% (DAE 9.13%-20.12% with analysis & admin fees); Premium 5.35%-14.75% (DAE 8.91%-19.87%).

## How to get it
Smart Mobile banner for personal loan (about 10 minutes, if eligible), or branch with ID. Documents: "doar actul tău de identitate și să semnezi acordul de consultare a veniturilor din baza de date ANAF"; the bank may ask for income documents (prudential requirements). Existing customers can get online pre-approval.

## Important
- The offer is indicative, not a firm offer: "Raiffeisen Bank SA poate să nu aprobe creditul".
- Recurring-income calculation excludes transfers between own Raiffeisen accounts, loan proceeds, cancelled transactions and interest from Raiffeisen savings products.
- Warn about total cost: DAE up to 39.4%. For debt consolidation see 'flexicredit-refinantare-fara-ipoteca'.
