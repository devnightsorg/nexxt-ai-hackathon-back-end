# Card de credit Raiffeisen Visa Platinum

## At a glance (structured data)
- **Slug:** card-credit-visa-platinum
- **Category:** credit_card
- **Summary:** Premium RON credit card (limit 5,000-90,000 lei) with free travel insurance, Business Lounge access, monthly cashback of 12.5 lei that offsets the 150-lei annual fee, and interest-free instalments.
- **Interest rate / return:** 26.0 % p.a. (revolving/cash balance) (as of 2026-10-28). DAE example 37.88% (5,367 lei, 12 months).
- **Minimum amount:** 5000 RON
- **Currencies:** RON
- **Good for:** travel, shopping, lounge_access

### Key features
- Limit 5,000 - 90,000 lei; daily cash limit 5,000 lei; grace up to 56 days
- Cashback 12.5 lei per cycle with purchases >= 1,500 lei - covers the annual administration fee
- Free complete travel insurance package; free Visa Business Lounge access; LoungeKey entries 30 EUR/person
- Up to 12 fixed interest-free or 60 reduced-interest instalments; 3/6/9 interest-free via Smart Mobile
- Annual fee 150 lei; interest 26% on revolving balance (DAE example 37.88%)

### Fees
- **Annual account administration fee (any number of cards):** 150 lei (offset by 12.5 lei monthly cashback if purchases >= 1,500 lei in a cycle) - Due at first debit/credit movement and yearly after
- **Issue / reissue / renewal fee:** 25 lei - Reissue not charged if at bank's initiative or damaged card
- **Merchant payments:** 0%
- **Cash withdrawal at Raiffeisen ATM:** 0%
- **Balance enquiry at Raiffeisen ATM:** 1 leu (free in Smart Mobile)
- **Penalty interest rate (rata dobânzii penalizatoare):** 3% p.a. per fee list - Fee list s.7; how it combines with the normal rate is defined in the contract (not retrieved)
- **Optional 'Protecția rambursărilor' insurance:** 0.5% of the highest credit used in the cycle (+ unpaid instalment part) - After a free period
- **Cash withdrawal other banks/abroad:** 1% + 2.5 lei - Fee list s.7 (grouping inferred)

### Eligibility
- Age 22-65
- Net family income at least the minimum net wage per economy
- At least 3 months at current workplace
- No executed loans / no loans repaid with delays
- Employees and pensioners can apply with ID only (no income certificate) - per page
