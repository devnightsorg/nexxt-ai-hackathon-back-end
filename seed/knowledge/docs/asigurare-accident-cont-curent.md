# Asigurare de viață în caz de accident (pentru titularii de cont curent)

## At a glance (structured data)
- **Slug:** asigurare-accident-cont-curent
- **Category:** insurance
- **Summary:** Low-cost accident cover for holders of a RON or EUR current account aged 18-64: 45,000 lei or 10,000 EUR paid on death or total permanent disability from accident, worldwide, for 6.75 lei or 1.50 EUR per month after the first free month.
- **Interest rate / return:** none or not applicable for this product (see details; any rate not found is stated as NOT FOUND)
- **Minimum amount:** 6.75 RON (per month)
- **Currencies:** RON, EUR
- **Terms:** Monthly renewable
- **Good for:** protection

### Key features
- Sum insured 45,000 lei (RON policy) or 10,000 EUR (EUR policy) for death from accident or total permanent disability from accident, worldwide
- Premium 6.75 lei/month or 1.50 EUR/month, first calendar month free, paid by automatic debit from the current account
- Eligibility: holder of a RON or EUR current account at Raiffeisen aged 18-64; enrolled on one current account
- Suspended in a month with unpaid premium; terminated after 6 consecutive unpaid months; can be cancelled any time (premiums not refunded)

### Fees
- **Monthly premium:** 6.75 lei or 1.50 EUR - After 1 free calendar month

### Eligibility
- Titular of a current account in RON or EUR at Raiffeisen Bank
- Age 18-64
- Enrolment in any branch

## Quoted facts
"Programul presupune o asigurare de viață pentru titularii de conturi curente în Lei sau în Euro ... acoperă decesul și invaliditatea totală permanentă din accident, oriunde în lume ... despăgubire de 45.000 Lei sau 10.000 Euro". Beneficiary: legal heir for death; the insured for permanent total invalidity. "Programul oferă 1 lună calendaristică de gratuitate ... costul lunar este de 6,75 Lei sau 1,50 Euro." Max suspension 6 consecutive months, then termination. Conditions document: 20260619.

Distribution: Raiffeisen Bank S.A. acts as affiliated agent (cod RAJ-500196, ASF) for UNIQA; insurance is optional unless stated; before signing the customer must read the pre-contractual information (IPID/KID), conditions and the bank's 'Intermediere asigurări' notice.
