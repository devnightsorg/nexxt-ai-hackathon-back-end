# Condiții Generale Bancare Persoane Fizice (CGB-PF) - highlights

Source: 'Condițiile Generale de Derulare a Operațiunilor Bancare - Persoane Fizice', edition 26.02.2026 (66 pages). Highlights below are paraphrased; clause numbers are from that edition.

## Account opening and customer duties (3.1)
- The customer must provide on request any information/documents the bank deems necessary to open and operate accounts, justify transactions, establish the purpose and source/destination of funds, tax residency and tax ID, incl. source of funds invested in virtual currencies (3.1.3).
- Bank may refuse accounts, refuse/limit payments, block accounts/cards/mobile banking, terminate the relationship if the client doesn't cooperate, suspicion of fraud/money laundering/sanctions breach, or the transaction does not fit the bank's risk profile (3.1.4). Limitations are notified in advance where possible.
- Accounts opened on non-working days are dated the next working day (3.1.6).

## Authorised persons (3.2)
Maximum **two** authorised persons (împuterniciți) per account; they can operate but not close accounts (exception: term deposits and savings accounts can be liquidated/closed by an authorised person). No authorised persons on credit-card accounts.

## Closing accounts (3.6)
- Customer may terminate a current account any time by written request in any branch, if no product needing the account is outstanding. For current accounts with a debit card, closing takes effect **30 calendar days** after the written request when transactions are pending settlement (3.6.1).
- The bank may terminate a current account, savings account, mobile banking or payment instrument with **two calendar months' written notice**, without giving reasons (3.6.2). Term deposits are terminated per the specific contract.

## Dormant accounts (3.7)
Current/savings accounts with no customer-approved change request and no debit/credit movement for a period set by the bank (fee list notes: no activity in last 6 months) become 'dormant'. The bank keeps charging fees while the balance is positive and keeps accruing interest; zero/negative dormant accounts may be closed without notice; the bank does not notify the transition; a transaction reactivates the account. Interest and fee postings made by the bank do not count as account movement.

## Payments (section 4) and unauthorised/incorrect payments (4.10)
- The customer bears losses from unauthorised transactions resulting from fraud or intentional / grossly negligent breach of payment-instrument obligations.
- **The bank corrects a payment only if the customer reports it within 30 days of the account statement date, but not later than 13 months from the debit date**; later reports must justify the delay (4.10.7). Refunds can be reversed by the bank if fraud or error by the client is later proven (4.10.5-6).
- Notifications received after 16:00 on a working day are treated as received on the next working day, except payment services with specific cut-off times (8.2.5).

## Amendments to the contract (8.x)
Changes are notified to the client at least **two months** before the proposed application date; **silence counts as tacit acceptance**, and the client may reject the change and terminate before the date (8.x; 7.x for payment services). Card-related clause changes also use 2 months' notice (6.x).

## Data and credit reporting
- The bank reports loans above the BNR reporting threshold (**20,000 lei cumulated exposure**) and arrears over 30 days to the **Centrala Riscului de Credit** (BNR) and processes data in the **Biroul de Credit** (data stored up to 4 years from update per the privacy policy). See 'gdpr-protectia-datelor'.
- FATCA and CRS: the bank applies FATCA and CRS and reports relevant account holders to ANAF.

## Complaints and disputes (8.3)
Romanian law; amicable settlement first; then out-of-court channels (CSALB, ANPC, BNR, ANSPDCP for data matters, ASF for investment services) or courts. See 'reclamatii-si-solutionarea-litigiilor'.

## Deposit guarantee (8.4)
The bank participates in FGDB; see 'fgdb-garantarea-depozitelor'.

## Language and law
Contracts and communications in Romanian; Romanian law applies.
