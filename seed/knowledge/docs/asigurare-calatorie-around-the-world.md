# Asigurare de călătorie anuală UNIQA Travel Around The World

## At a glance (structured data)
- **Slug:** asigurare-calatorie-around-the-world
- **Category:** insurance
- **Summary:** Annual multi-trip travel insurance bought only in Smart Mobile: 149 lei/year for one person or 268 lei/year for you plus up to 4 co-insured; medical cover up to 30,000 EUR per insured per year, up to 45 days per trip.
- **Interest rate / return:** none or not applicable for this product (see details; any rate not found is stated as NOT FOUND)
- **Minimum amount:** not specified / see details
- **Currencies:** RON
- **Terms:** 1 year
- **Good for:** travel

### Key features
- 149 lei/year (1 person) or 268 lei/year (you + up to 4 co-insured, max 5 insured)
- Unlimited trips abroad during the year, each up to 45 consecutive days; worldwide except Romania and country of citizenship/residence
- Medical treatment abroad up to 30,000 EUR per insured per year; emergency dental; medical repatriation; amateur sports; luggage/flight/documents; road assistance up to 1,750 EUR; civil liability 5,000 EUR; trip cancellation (storno)
- Optional clauses: extreme sports; accident cover
- Can be activated within 3 days even after leaving the country; 24/7 assistance via CORIS Assistance

### Fees
- **Premium - individual:** 149 lei per year
- **Premium - family (you + up to 4 co-insured):** 268 lei per year - Page also says 'maximum 5 insured'

### Eligibility
- Raiffeisen Smart Mobile user (product available exclusively in the app)
