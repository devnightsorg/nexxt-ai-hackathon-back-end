# Depozit la termen în LEI

## At a glance (structured data)
- **Slug:** depozit-termen-lei
- **Category:** term_deposit
- **Summary:** Fixed-rate RON term deposits opened 100% in Smart Mobile: 3 months 4.70%, 12 months 5.20%, 24 months 6.40% (standard rates); 0% interest if broken early (except 24-month deposits).
- **Interest rate / return:** 5.2 % p.a. fixed (12 months) (as of 2026-10-28). Standard RON rates via Smart Mobile: 3m 4.70% (auto-renew), 12m 5.20% (auto-renew), 24m 6.40%. 10% tax withheld at source on interest.
- **Minimum amount:** 500 RON
- **Currencies:** RON
- **Terms:** 3 months; 12 months; 24 months
- **Good for:** short_term_saving, home_purchase, fixed_return

### Key features
- Standard fixed rates: 3 months 4.70%; 12 months 5.20%; 24 months 6.40%
- Minimum 500 lei; opened exclusively via Raiffeisen Smart Mobile at these rates
- Interest capitalised at maturity; automatic renewal for 3- and 12-month deposits
- 0% opening, administration and closing commission
- Early liquidation: 3-12 month deposits earn 0.00% interest; 24-month deposits earn 5.00% if broken before 12 months and 5.50% between 12 and 24 months
- Salary customers can get higher 'bonus' rates (see depozit-bonus-dobanda-incasari)

### Fees
- **Opening / administration / closing:** 0% - Page: 'Ai 0% comision pentru deschiderea, administrarea și închiderea depozitului'
- **Early liquidation interest (3-12 months):** 0.00% interest paid - No interest is paid if liquidated before maturity
- **Early liquidation interest (24 months):** 5.00% if before 12 months; 5.50% between 12 and 24 months - Rate table row 'Dobândă bonificată în cazul lichidării...'
- **Tax on interest:** 10% withheld

### Eligibility
- Individual with a Raiffeisen current account (new customers open one in Smart Mobile in about 8 min)
- Calculator range: 500 - 1,000,000 lei

## Early liquidation (quoted)
"Dobânda bonificată în cazul lichidării depozitelor la termen în Lei înainte de maturitate: 3 - 12 luni - lichidate înainte de maturitate: 0.00%. 24 luni - lichidate înainte de 12 luni de la constituire: 5.00%; lichidate între 12 luni și 24 de luni de la constituire: 5.50%."

## Mechanics
- "Depozite la termen, cu dobândă fixă și capitalizare: ... Capitalizarea dobânzii la scadență. Reînnoire automată."
- Interest is paid at maturity by adding to the account balance (capitalising deposits) or by transfer to the current account (single-maturity deposits).
- Tax: "Plata dobânzii se efectuează după reținerea impozitului de 10% pe venituri din dobânzi".
- Guaranteed by FGDB up to RON equivalent of EUR 100,000 per depositor per bank.
- Open and liquidate in real time in Smart Mobile / Raiffeisen Online.

## Agent guidance (inferred)
Good when the user has a lump sum not needed for the term. Warn that breaking a 3-12 month deposit loses all interest. Compare with Flexidepozit (partial withdrawal) and Super Acces Plus (instant access) for money with uncertain needs.
