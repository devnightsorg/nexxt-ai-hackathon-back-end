# Credit ipotecar Casa Ta

## At a glance (structured data)
- **Slug:** credit-ipotecar-casa-ta
- **Category:** mortgage
- **Summary:** RON mortgage for buying, building or renovating a home or land: 3 to 30 years, 5,000-300,000 EUR equivalent, minimum 15% down payment; fixed rate 3 years from 5.05% or 5 years from 5.65% (Premium customers with life insurance), then IRCC + 2.30%.
- **Interest rate / return:** 5.05 % p.a. fixed 3 years (then variable IRCC + 2.30%) (as of 2026-10-28). Lowest rate: Premium customers (>=2,000 EUR monthly income received in Raiffeisen account over last 3 months, or >=40,000 EUR assets with Raiffeisen Group, Premium package) with life insurance via Raiffeisen/Uniqa (0.2% discount). Standard offer 6.50% fixed 3 years then IRCC+3.75%. 5-year fixed from 5.65% (Premium). Cost sheet dated 22.10.2026; IRCC 5.57% valid 01.10.2026.
- **Minimum amount:** 5000 EUR (equivalent in RON)
- **Currencies:** RON
- **Terms:** 3 - 30 years
- **Good for:** home_purchase, home_construction, home_renovation

### Key features
- 3-year fixed: from 5.05%, then variable IRCC + 2.30% (7.87% at IRCC 5.57%); 5-year fixed: from 5.65%, then IRCC + 2.30%
- Standard (non-Premium) offer: 3-year fixed 6.50%, then IRCC + 3.75% (9.32%)
- Amount 5,000 - 300,000 EUR equivalent in RON; term 3-30 years; down payment minimum 15%
- Pre-approval in max 24 hours, valid 45 days; first instalment can start in the 2nd month; 3 months payment pause any time after the first 6 months
- Salary into a Raiffeisen account gives a 1% interest reduction on RON loans (FAQ)
- Wide income acceptance: salary, pension, rent, independent activity; optional life insurance

### Fees
- **Analysis fee:** 900 lei
- **Property valuation fee:** 605 lei apartment / 787 lei house (VAT incl.) - Indicative, per valuation report
- **Mandatory property insurance (PAD):** 130 lei - Plus optional voluntary home insurance 0.11% x property value (annual premium rate used in examples)
- **Current-account package:** 3 lei/month (Standard package)
- **Life insurance (optional, example):** 0.026% of loan value per month - Used in DAE examples; gives 0.2% rate discount
- **Early repayment commission:** 0 (inferred from cost-sheet layout; verify) - Cost sheet lists 'Comision rambursare anticipată' followed by '0'
- **Notary and land-registry costs:** Paid by client; not in DAE

### Eligibility
- Age 21 to 63 (women) / 65 (men) at final maturity - applies to applicant and co-debtors with income
- Income at least the minimum net wage per economy for each participant
- At least 3 months at current workplace (permanent; fixed-term covering the loan; or fixed-term with >=2 successive renewals)
- No executed loans / loans repaid with major repeated delays
- Documents: ID, ANAF income consent (applicant + co-debtor), property documents (antecontract, ownership, land-book extract, valuation documents)
- Meeting criteria does not give a right to the loan; bank may refuse without detailed reasons

## Benefits (quoted)
Preaprobare financiară pe loc sau în maximum 24 ore, valabilă până la 45 de zile; 3 luni pauză la plata ratelor oricând după primele 6 luni; prima lună fără rată; opțional asigurare de viață; payment via mobile/internet banking; rate date can be changed free of charge.
FAQ: salary received in a Raiffeisen account gives "o reducere de 1% din rata dobânzii pentru un credit în Lei".

## Risks (bank warning, quoted in essence)
After the fixed period the rate follows the quarterly IRCC (updated 4 times a year); income fluctuation and IRCC rises can increase instalments; FX risk if the loan currency differs from income currency.
