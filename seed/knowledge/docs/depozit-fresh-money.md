# Depozit Fresh Money (sume noi, 4 luni)

## At a glance (structured data)
- **Slug:** depozit-fresh-money
- **Category:** term_deposit
- **Summary:** Promotional 4-month deposit for NEW money brought to Raiffeisen: 6.20% p.a. in RON and 2.50% in EUR (the bank markets these as its best rates, although its 24-month RON deposit pays 6.40% in the same table); closes at maturity, 0% interest if broken early.
- **Interest rate / return:** 6.2 % p.a. fixed (RON, 4 months) (as of 2026-10-28). RON 6.20% / EUR 2.50%; applies only to new money; 4 months; closes at maturity; promotional offer - check the page for current validity.
- **Minimum amount:** 500 RON
- **Currencies:** RON, EUR
- **Terms:** 4 months
- **Good for:** short_term_saving, fixed_return, bring_new_money

### Key features
- 4-month fixed deposit; RON 6.20% p.a., EUR 2.50% p.a.
- Only for 'new money' (sume noi): increase in total holdings of at least 500 lei / 100 EUR vs baseline date
- Minimum 500 lei / 100 EUR; maximum per client 1,000,000 lei / 200,000 EUR across all Fresh Money deposits; open as many as you like within the cap
- Opened 100% in Smart Mobile; no auto-renewal; interest paid at maturity to current account after tax
- Early liquidation: 0.00% interest
- Fees: 0% opening, administration and closing

### Fees
- **Opening / administration / closing:** 0%
- **Early liquidation interest (total liquidation):** 0.00%
- **Maximum exposure:** 1,000,000 lei / 200,000 EUR per client - Sum of all Fresh Money deposits
- **Tax on interest:** 10% withheld

### Eligibility
- Individual with a Raiffeisen current account (new customers can open one in 10-20 minutes in Smart Mobile)
- 'Sume noi': any amount >= 500 lei / 100 EUR by which total balance today exceeds total balance on the baseline date minus Fresh Money deposits already opened

## Rates and terms (quoted)
- Maturity: 4 months. Rate: 6.20% RON / 2.50% EUR, fixed. Minimum: 500 lei / 100 EUR. Maximum: "Se pot deschide oricâte depozite Fresh Money dar în limita a maximum 1,000,000 lei / 200,000 Eur / client". Closing at maturity (no renewal). Early total liquidation: 0.00% interest.
- Interest capitalised/paid at maturity "prin virarea soldului depozitului în contul curent, numai după reținerea impozitului pe venituri din dobânzi".

## What counts as "new money" (quoted formula)
"Sume noi = orice sumă >= cu 500 lei / 100 Eur, calculată astfel: Soldul total astăzi – Soldul total la [data de bază] – Soldul depozitelor Fresh Money deja deschise." "Soldul total" = sum of balances in current accounts, savings accounts, deposits at Raiffeisen AND investment funds managed by Raiffeisen Asset Management. Balance growth must come from interbank or intrabank transfers from other customers (NOT transfers between the customer's own Raiffeisen accounts) or cash deposits at Raiffeisen MFMs at the time the deposit is opened.

## Data inconsistencies on the bank's pages (flag)
The baseline date is written as 04.09.2026 on the Fresh Money page, as "4-Sep-2026 ... 04.08.2026" (typo inside the same formula) on the term-deposit page and as "6 mai 2026" on the savings hub page. Use the Fresh Money product page (04.09.2026) and verify in the app.

## Marketing claim vs table
The page says 6.20% is "cea mai bună dobândă la lei din oferta Raiffeisen Bank", but the standard rate table lists 6.40% for the 24-month RON deposit - so Fresh Money is the best rate only for short maturities (4 months).

## Guidance (inferred)
Best for users who can move savings from another bank for exactly 4 months. NOT for money already held at Raiffeisen (moving funds from the customer's own Raiffeisen current/savings accounts does not count). Caution: breaking early forfeits all interest.
