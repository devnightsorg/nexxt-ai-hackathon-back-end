# Carduri de debit Visa EUR și Visa USD

## At a glance (structured data)
- **Slug:** card-debit-visa-eur-usd
- **Category:** debit_card
- **Summary:** Visa debit cards on EUR or USD accounts: no currency-conversion fee when paying in the euro area (EUR card), 5 EUR/USD issue fee and 10 EUR/USD annual fee.
- **Interest rate / return:** none or not applicable for this product (see details; any rate not found is stated as NOT FOUND)
- **Minimum amount:** 50 EUR/USD (initial deposit outside a package)
- **Currencies:** EUR, USD
- **Good for:** travel, fx_spending

### Key features
- EUR card: no conversion fees for payments in euro-area countries
- USD card: better FX rate between current accounts Mon-Fri 8:30-18:00 (page)
- Contactless Visa, 3D Secure; Smart Hour for RON<->EUR

### Fees
- **Issue / replacement (EUR / USD):** 5 EUR / 5 USD
- **Annual administration:** 10 EUR / 10 USD
- **Delivery:** 3 EUR / 3.50 USD
- **Minimum initial deposit:** 50 EUR / 50 USD - Outside a package
- **ATM cash:** 1.5% min 10 lei equivalent

### Eligibility
- Individuals (Romanian or foreign citizens incl. diplomatic missions / international organisations) with a Raiffeisen current account

Pentru tranzacțiile făcute cu acest card în zona Euro nu sunt percepute taxe de conversie valutară'; USD card similar with 'schimb valutar la un curs mai avantajos ... între conturile curente, de luni până vineri, de la 8:30 la 18:00'.
