# Credit ipotecar Noua Casă (program guvernamental, prima locuință)

## At a glance (structured data)
- **Slug:** credit-ipotecar-noua-casa
- **Category:** mortgage
- **Summary:** State-guaranteed first-home mortgage (FNGCIMM) in RON at variable IRCC + 2% (7.57%), financing up to 95% of homes priced up to 70,000 EUR (85% for new/consolidated homes 70,001-140,000 EUR), 3-30 years; lending resumed on 25.05.2026 under the 2026 ceiling.
- **Interest rate / return:** 7.57 % p.a. variable (IRCC + 2%) (as of 2026-10-28). Formula IRCC + 2%; IRCC 5.56% for Q1 2026 used in the cost sheet dated 01.10.2026 -> 7.57%. DAE computed for 230,000 lei over 30 years: 8.23% / 8.25%.
- **Minimum amount:** not specified / see details
- **Currencies:** RON
- **Terms:** 3 - 30 years
- **Good for:** home_purchase, first_home

### Key features
- Variable rate IRCC + 2% = 7.57% (DAE 8.23% / 8.25%); FNGCIMM management fee 0.15% per year on the state-guaranteed balance
- Financing up to 95% (price <= 70,000 EUR; new or old) or 85% (price 70,001-140,000 EUR; new/consolidated only); down payment 5% or 15%
- Maximum loan: up to 66,500 EUR equivalent (price <= 70,000 EUR) or 59,501 - 119,000 EUR equivalent (new/consolidated, price 70,001 - 140,000 EUR)
- State guarantee 50% (price <= 70,000 EUR) or 60% (70,001-140,000 EUR); collateral deposit equal to 3 interest instalments
- Term 3-30 years; pre-approval valid 45 days; only completed, land-registered homes

### Fees
- **FNGCIMM management commission:** 0.15% per year on the state-guaranteed share of the balance
- **Property valuation:** 605 lei apartment / 787 lei house (VAT incl.)
- **Standard current-account package:** 3 lei/month
- **PAD property insurance:** 130 lei + premium rate for property insurance - Only property insurance is mandatory
- **Collateral deposit:** Equal to 3 interest instalments

### Eligibility
- Age 21 to 63 (women) / 65 (men) at maturity; income >= minimum net wage per participant; >= 3 months at current job
- At application you do not own (alone or with spouse) any home, OR own at most one home, acquired other than through Noua Casă, with usable area under 50 sq m - proven by a notarised sworn statement
- No executed loans / no large repeated delays
- Documents: ID, ANAF consent, notarised declaration; later antecontract, ownership documents, land-book extract, energy certificate
