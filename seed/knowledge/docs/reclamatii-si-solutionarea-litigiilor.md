# Complaints procedure and dispute resolution (reclamații, CSALB, ANPC, ASF)

## How to file a complaint (bank information sheet dated 2023-07-17; check that it is still current)
- Call Center **\*2000** (Mon-Fri 8:00-22:00, Sat 9:00-17:30)
- E-mail **centrala@raiffeisen.ro**
- Website form 'Opinia ta contează' (PF: raiffeisen.ro/persoane-fizice/in-sprijinul-tau/opinia-ta-conteaza/)
- Secure message in Raiffeisen Online (internet banking)
- Any branch
- Post: Raiffeisen Bank SA, Direcția Dezvoltare Vânzări și Service, Sky Tower, Calea Floreasca nr. 246C, 014476 București (response time extends by postal time)
Provide: description, name, CNP, phone, e-mail, correspondence address, relevant documents and what you want the bank to do. You receive a **registration number by SMS**.

## Maximum response times (quoted)
- **Payment-related complaints: 15 working days**, extendable by a further 20 working days in exceptional cases
- **Personal-data protection complaints: one month**, extendable up to 3 months
- **Other complaints: 30 days**, extendable up to 45 days
- Complaints about investment services: no separate deadline appears in the extracted sheet (a search-engine summary mentioned 30 days + max 15 days, but that was NOT found in the PDF text - treat as UNVERIFIED); CGB 8.3.2 refers investment-service disputes to ASF or BNR
If analysis takes longer the bank informs the customer. If the answer does not satisfy the client, the bank's reply must refer to out-of-court bodies.

## Out-of-court and regulatory bodies (quoted contact data)
- **CSALB** - Centrul de Soluționare Alternativă a Litigiilor din Domeniul Bancar, Str. Sevastopol 24, et. 2, sector 1, București, tel. **021 9414**, www.csalb.ro, office@csalb.ro. Free for consumers; resolution time max **90 days** (OG 38/2015).
- **SAL-FIN** (ASF alternative dispute resolution for non-bank financial) - Splaiul Independenței 15, București, office@salfin.ro, www.salfin.ro.
- **ANPC** - Autoritatea Națională pentru Protecția Consumatorilor, B-dul Aviatorilor 72, sector 1, București, www.anpc.ro (SAL platform: reclamatiisal.anpc.ro).
- **BNR** - Str. Lipscani 25, sector 3, București (lending, payment services, deposits).
- **ASF** - Splaiul Independenței 15, București, tel. 0800.825.627 / 021.305.3470 (investment services and products; insurance).
- **ANSPDCP** - data protection (see 'gdpr-protectia-datelor').
- Mediation under the mediation law, or courts. Romanian law applies; first an amicable settlement attempt (CGB 8.3).

## Unauthorised / wrongly executed payments
Report within 30 days of the statement and no later than 13 months from debit date (CGB 4.10.7). Block a lost/stolen card immediately from Smart Mobile or *2000.
