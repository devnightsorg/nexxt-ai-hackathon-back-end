# Card de credit Raiffeisen eMAG

## At a glance (structured data)
- **Slug:** card-credit-emag
- **Category:** credit_card
- **Summary:** Co-branded RON credit card (limit 700-50,000 lei) with eMAG loyalty points (up to 10% back on eMAG orders), up to 24 fixed interest-free instalments; annual fee 60 lei, 26% interest on revolving balance.
- **Interest rate / return:** 26.0 % p.a. (revolving/cash balance) (as of 2026-10-28). DAE example 33.37% (5,367 lei over 12 months at 26%); 0% on merchant purchases repaid in full within the grace period.
- **Minimum amount:** 700 RON
- **Currencies:** RON
- **Terms:** Instalments 3-60 months
- **Good for:** shopping, online_shopping, instalment_purchases

### Key features
- eMAG loyalty points: up to 10% back in points on fully-paid eMAG orders
- Up to 24 fixed interest-free instalments (or up to 60 reduced-interest) and 3/6/9 interest-free instalments in Smart Mobile
- Limit 700 - 50,000 lei; grace period up to 56 days; minimum repayment 5% (min 10 lei)
- Annual fee 60 lei; interest 26% on revolving balance (DAE example 33.37%)
- Yellow Friday promo: 150 lei bonus on >=1,000 lei transactions (20.10 - 02.11.2026)

### Fees
- **Annual account administration fee (any number of cards):** 60 lei - Due at first debit/credit movement and yearly after
- **Issue / reissue / renewal fee:** 25 lei - Reissue not charged if at bank's initiative or damaged card
- **Merchant payments:** 0%
- **Cash withdrawal at Raiffeisen ATM:** 0%
- **Balance enquiry at Raiffeisen ATM:** 1 leu (free in Smart Mobile)
- **Penalty interest rate (rata dobânzii penalizatoare):** 3% p.a. per fee list - Fee list s.7; how it combines with the normal rate is defined in the contract (not retrieved)
- **Optional 'Protecția rambursărilor' insurance:** 0.5% of the highest credit used in the cycle (+ unpaid instalment part) - After a free period
- **Cash withdrawal at other banks / abroad:** 1% + 2.5 lei - Fee list s.7

### Eligibility
- Age 22-65
- Net family income at least the minimum net wage per economy
- At least 3 months at current workplace
- No executed loans / no loans repaid with delays
- Employees and pensioners can apply with ID only (no income certificate) - per page

## Quoted facts
"Cumperi de la eMAG tot ce ai nevoie ... și noi te răsplătim cu până la 10% înapoi din valoarea cumpărăturilor, în puncte, pentru comenzile plătite integral" (loyalty regulation AA1-AA3 PDF not retrieved - exact point conditions unknown). Up to 24 fixed interest-free instalments or up to 60 fixed reduced-interest instalments.
Cost examples: DAE 33.37% for 5,367 lei repaid over 12 months at 26%. Annual fee 60 lei (fee list). Same limit/eligibility as Visa Standard.
