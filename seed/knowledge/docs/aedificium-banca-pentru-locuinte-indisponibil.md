# Housing savings (Banca pentru Locuințe / Aedificium BpL) - discontinued

**Do NOT recommend a Raiffeisen housing-savings (Bauspar / economisire-creditare în domeniul locativ) product. It is no longer sold.**

## What happened (sources)
- The housing-savings subsidiary, formerly *Raiffeisen Banca pentru Locuințe*, was renamed **Aedificium Banca pentru Locuințe (ABL)** (the bank's own legal definitions in the CGB still list it as a Group entity; the Raiffeisen group page describes it as "Servicii de economisire-creditare pentru domeniul locativ. A fost prima bancă de acest tip din sistemul bancar românesc", www.rbl.ro).
- **28 November 2023**: ABL shareholders decided to give up the banking licence (dissolution followed by voluntary liquidation). Raiffeisen Bank reported it to BVB/ASF on 29.11.2023: "ABL își va îndeplini integral toate obligațiile față de clienți și creditori"; sums for clients who cannot be reached would be deposited with Raiffeisen Bank România for collection.
- **June 2025**: BNR approved the dissolution, liquidation and withdrawal of the licence (Profit.ro, 24 June 2025: BNR Board found ABL had enough funds to pay creditors). A search summary of the ABL site cites BNR decision no. 16 of 11.06.2025 and that ABL ceased activity as of 23.06.2025 (sources conflict on exact dates; the ABL site was behind Cloudflare and could not be read directly).
- At end of 9M/2023 the bank had only about 500 active deposits worth 6.5 million lei (ZF.ro).
- Raiffeisen Bank România was the majority shareholder (99.9993%).

## What the agent can say
- New housing-savings contracts cannot be opened; for saving towards a home use a savings account, term deposits or a mortgage (Casa Ta / Noua Casă) - see 'cont-economii-super-acces-plus', 'depozit-termen-lei', 'credit-ipotecar-casa-ta'.
- Existing contract holders should contact ABL / Raiffeisen (*2000) for settlement of sums due; state-premium rules and rates for old contracts were NOT retrieved (historical figures from 2012-2014 press: state premium up to 25% of annual savings, savings interest 3% - not current, do not quote).
