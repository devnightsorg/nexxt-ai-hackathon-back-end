# Asigurări de viață atașate creditelor (optionale)

## At a glance (structured data)
- **Slug:** asigurare-viata-atasata-creditelor
- **Category:** insurance
- **Summary:** Optional life (and unemployment/hospitalisation/invalidity) insurance linked to Raiffeisen loans, cards and overdraft; premium single or monthly; on Flexicredit it gives a 2% rate discount; benefit pays the loan balance to the bank.
- **Interest rate / return:** none or not applicable for this product (see details; any rate not found is stated as NOT FOUND)
- **Minimum amount:** not specified / see details
- **Currencies:** RON
- **Terms:** Loan term
- **Good for:** protection, debt_protection

### Key features
- Optional for all credit types (personal loans, mortgages, credit cards, overdraft)
- Risks (depends on plan): death from any cause, hospitalisation, unemployment, total permanent invalidity
- Flexicredit with optional life & unemployment insurance: 2% lower interest rate
- Premium paid once for the whole loan period or monthly from the account associated with the loan
- Beneficiary is the bank up to the outstanding debt; any excess goes to you or your heirs

### Fees
- **Premium - Flexicredit:** Single premium = loan value x 0.12% x loan months (financed) - Cost sheet footnote
- **Premium - mortgages:** 0.026% of loan value per month (example) - Casa Ta cost sheet example

### Eligibility
- Taken when contracting the loan in any branch; check you hold a 'Certificat de asigurare' if already a borrower

## Quoted facts
"Asigurarea de viață atașată creditelor acordate de bancă este opțională. Practic poți decide individual dacă dorești să beneficiezi de protecția oferită." Monthly-premium policies are suspended in a month without payment; reinstated by paying; after a maximum suspension period they terminate. Insurer: UNIQA Asigurări de Viață. Product information documents (PID) exist for credit cards, mortgages, personal loans and overdraft (not retrieved).

Distribution: Raiffeisen Bank S.A. acts as affiliated agent (cod RAJ-500196, ASF) for UNIQA; insurance is optional unless stated; before signing the customer must read the pre-contractual information (IPID/KID), conditions and the bank's 'Intermediere asigurări' notice.
