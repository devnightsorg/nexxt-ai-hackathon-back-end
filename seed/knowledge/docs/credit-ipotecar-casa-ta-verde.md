# Credit ipotecar Casa Ta Verde

## At a glance (structured data)
- **Slug:** credit-ipotecar-casa-ta-verde
- **Category:** mortgage
- **Summary:** Green mortgage for energy-efficient homes (RoGBC Green Homes or energy class A/B issued after 15.02.2023): fixed 4.85% for 3 years or 5.45% for 5 years for Premium customers, then IRCC + 2.10%; same limits as Casa Ta.
- **Interest rate / return:** 4.85 % p.a. fixed 3 years (then variable IRCC + 2.10%) (as of 2026-10-28). Premium customers with life insurance; property meeting green criteria. Standard offer 6.30% (3y) / 6.90% (5y). Cost sheets 20-22.10.2026; IRCC 5.57%.
- **Minimum amount:** 5000 EUR (equivalent in RON)
- **Currencies:** RON
- **Terms:** 3 - 30 years
- **Good for:** home_purchase, green_home

### Key features
- 3-year fixed from 4.85%, then variable IRCC + 2.10% (7.67%); 5-year fixed from 5.45%, then IRCC + 2.10%
- Standard offer: 3-year fixed 6.30%, 5-year fixed 6.90%, then IRCC + 3.55% (9.12%)
- Property must hold Green Homes (RoGBC) certification or energy class A / superior / B (certificate issued after 15.02.2023)
- Amount 5,000-300,000 EUR equivalent; term 3-30 years; minimum 15% down payment
- Early repayment commission shown as 0 in the 3-year cost sheet

### Fees
- **Analysis fee:** 900 lei
- **Valuation fee:** 605 lei apartment / 787 lei house (VAT incl.)
- **PAD insurance:** 130 lei - Optional home insurance 0.11% x value
- **Standard current-account package:** 3 lei/month
- **Early repayment commission:** 0 (3-year cost sheet)

### Eligibility
- Age 21 to 63 (women) / 65 (men) at final maturity - applies to applicant and co-debtors with income
- Income at least the minimum net wage per economy for each participant
- At least 3 months at current workplace (permanent; fixed-term covering the loan; or fixed-term with >=2 successive renewals)
- No executed loans / loans repaid with major repeated delays
- Documents: ID, ANAF income consent (applicant + co-debtor), property documents (antecontract, ownership, land-book extract, valuation documents)
- Meeting criteria does not give a right to the loan; bank may refuse without detailed reasons
