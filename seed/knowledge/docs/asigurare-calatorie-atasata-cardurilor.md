# Asigurare de călătorie gratuită atașată cardurilor Platinum / Signature / Infinite

## At a glance (structured data)
- **Slug:** asigurare-calatorie-atasata-cardurilor
- **Category:** insurance
- **Summary:** Free travel insurance (UNIQA, CORIS assistance) for holders of Visa Platinum, Signature or Infinite debit cards and Visa Credit Platinum, Signature or Infinite cards: unlimited trips per year, up to 60 consecutive days per trip, family covered.
- **Interest rate / return:** none or not applicable for this product (see details; any rate not found is stated as NOT FOUND)
- **Minimum amount:** not specified / see details
- **Currencies:** RON
- **Terms:** Per card validity
- **Good for:** travel

### Key features
- Free for Visa Platinum/Signature/Infinite debit and Visa Credit Platinum/Signature/Infinite cards
- Unlimited trips in a year, each up to 60 consecutive days; valid worldwide except Romania/country of citizenship or residence; family members covered
- Covers organisation and payment of emergency medical services and travel expenses
- Standard/eMAG credit card holders who bought the optional policy before 12 July 2024 keep it under their original terms (50 lei/year)

### Fees
- **Cost:** 0 lei for eligible cards - Fee list s.7C: 'Gratuit' for the eligible cards

### Eligibility
- Hold one of the listed Raiffeisen cards
- Insurance becomes active when leaving Romania / country of residence
