## Common facts for Raiffeisen Asset Management (SAI RAM) open-end funds
- Manager: S.A.I. Raiffeisen Asset Management S.A. (ASF register PJR05SAIR/400019/08.02.2006), Calea Floreasca 246 D, tel. 021.306.1711, www.raiffeisenfonduri.ro. Depositary: Raiffeisen Bank S.A. Distributed by Raiffeisen Bank (branches and Smart Mobile: Produse -> Fonduri de investiții).
- NOT a bank deposit: "investițiile în fondurile de investiții nu sunt depozite bancare, nefiind garantate de Fondul de Garantare a Depozitelor Bancare"; capital can be partly or fully lost; past performance is no guarantee; local funds are not covered by an investor compensation fund for market losses.
- Redemption on request on any business day (no lock-up); minimum initial subscription = value of one fund unit; later any amount. Recurring investing via SmartInvest from 200 RON / 50 EUR / 50 USD per month.
- Taxation: gains on redemptions are taxed at source by SAI RAM (the client does not file Declarația Unică for them); the Raiffeisen page refers to the Fiscal Code for the rate and does not state the percentage (rate NOT quoted here).
- Before subscribing the customer must read the prospectus and the Key Information Document (DIE/KID) at raiffeisenfonduri.ro (Informare / Documente și rapoarte fonduri) and the bank's MiFID presentation document; the bank provides investment services WITHOUT advice and applies an appropriateness test (see 'mifid-profil-risc-adecvare-investitii').
