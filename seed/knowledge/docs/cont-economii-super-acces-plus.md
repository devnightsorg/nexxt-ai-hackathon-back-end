# Cont de economii Super Acces Plus

## At a glance (structured data)
- **Slug:** cont-economii-super-acces-plus
- **Category:** savings_account
- **Summary:** Flexible instant-access savings account in RON, EUR or USD with tiered variable interest (RON 2.00%/2.50%/3.00% by balance band), no monthly fee, deposits/withdrawals anytime and interest paid daily-accrued, monthly-capitalised.
- **Interest rate / return:** 2.0 % p.a. (RON, band up to 50,000 lei; variable) (as of 2026-10-28). RON: <=50,000 lei 2.00%; 50,001-300,000 lei 2.50%; >300,000 lei 3.00%. EUR 0.50% flat; USD 0.30% flat. Rates are variable and differentiated per balance band (each slice earns its band's rate). Interest income is taxed 10% at source.
- **Minimum amount:** 1 RON
- **Currencies:** RON, EUR, USD
- **Terms:** No fixed term (unlimited)
- **Good for:** emergency_fund, short_term_saving, travel, child_education

### Key features
- RON interest tiers: up to 50,000 lei 2.00%; 50,001-300,000 lei 2.50%; above 300,000 lei 3.00% (marginal bands)
- EUR 0.50% and USD 0.30% regardless of balance
- Variable rate; interest accrues daily on end-of-day balance, credited monthly on the last day of the month; new deposits earn interest from the day received
- 0 lei administration, 0% opening and closing commission
- Open 100% online from Smart Mobile in RON, EUR or USD; no minimum opening amount stated (1 leu/EUR/USD)
- Free automatic monthly transfers from salary/current account through FixPay
- Deposit cash at Raiffeisen multifunctional machines (MFM) with a Raiffeisen card
- See interest received in Smart Mobile (useful for annual income declaration)

### Fees
- **Account opening:** 0 lei / EUR / USD - Fee list s.1.2
- **Monthly administration:** 0 lei (RON) / 0 EUR/USD - Fee list s.1.2
- **Card cash withdrawal from savings account:** 1.5%, min 10 lei (min 1 EUR/USD) - Standard debit-card cash fee depending on the holder's package
- **Withholding tax on interest:** 10% - Deducted before interest is paid, per the pages

### Eligibility
- Account holder aged 18+ (hub page: 'Vârsta titularului: minimum 18 ani')
- Existing customers open in Smart Mobile; non-customers first open a current account (about 8 minutes, 5 steps)

## What it is
"Contul de Economii Super Acces este varianta flexibilă a unui depozit bancar, are avantajul unei dobânzi comparabile cu cea a unui depozit la termen și flexibilitatea unui cont curent pentru depuneri și retrageri." Commercial name on the fee list: "Contul de economii Super Acces Plus".

## Rates (quoted, retrieved 2026-10-28)
| Currency | Balance | Annual rate |
|---|---|---|
| RON | <= 50,000 | 2.00% |
| RON | 50,001 - 300,000 | 2.50% |
| RON | > 300,000 | 3.00% |
| EUR | any | 0.50% |
| USD | any | 0.30% |

Rate is variable (fluctuantă) and tiered: "pentru fiecare fracțiune din suma depusă care se încadrează într-o tranșă valorică se aplică rata dobânzii aferentă respectivei tranșe". Example from the page: 400,000 lei -> first 50,000 at tier 1, next 250,000 at tier 2, remaining 100,000 at tier 3. Interest is calculated daily on the end-of-day balance (truncated to 2 decimals), capitalised monthly on the last day of the month. The hub page says "până la 3% pe an pentru LEI".

## Key parameters
- Currency: RON, EUR, USD. Minimum opening amount: 1 leu / 1 EUR / 1 USD (hub page: "fără sumă minimă de deschidere"). The online calculator uses 500 as its default minimum - that is a calculator limit, not a product rule.
- Term: unlimited. Partial withdrawals and additional deposits: anytime, any amount.
- Holder age: minimum 18.
- Access: Smart Mobile, Raiffeisen Online (internet banking), transfers in real time. Deposit cash at MFMs with a Raiffeisen card. For cash withdrawals via debit card: standard fee 1.5% (min 10 lei) depending on package benefits.
- FixPay: free automatic monthly transfers from salary/current account into the savings account.
- Tax: "Plata dobânzii în contul tău se face după reținerea impozitului de 10% pe venitul din dobânzi".
- Guarantee: covered by FGDB up to the RON equivalent of EUR 100,000 per depositor per bank.

## When to recommend (agent guidance, inferred)
Emergency fund (3-6 monthly salaries - the bank itself cites "fond de urgență de minimum 3-6 salarii"), short-term goals (holiday, car down payment), money that must stay instantly available. For idle balances above the emergency buffer, compare with term deposits (see 'depozite-la-termen').
