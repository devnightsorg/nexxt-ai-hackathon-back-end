# IRCC and variable-rate loans (Casa Ta, overdraft)

- **IRCC** (Indicele de Referință pentru Creditele Consumatorilor) is the quarterly reference index for RON consumer loans. Per the bank: it is "calculat ca medie aritmetică a ratelor dobânzilor zilnice ale tranzacțiilor pe piața monetară interbancară pentru trimestrul 2 din 2026: **5.57%** (valoare valabilă la 01.10.2026)". The Noua Casă cost sheet (dated 01.10.2026) refers to the quarter-1-2026 value **5.56%**. IRCC is updated 4 times a year, applied from the first calendar day of the following quarter.
- Variable-rate formula: bank rate = IRCC + bank margin. Examples valid 01.10.2026: Casa Ta Premium: IRCC 5.57% + 2.30% = 7.87%; Casa Ta standard: + 3.75% = 9.32%; Casa Ta Verde Premium + 2.10% = 7.67%; standard + 3.55% = 9.12%; Noua Casă IRCC + 2% = 7.57%; overdraft IRCC + 9.99% = 15.56% (IRCC + 4.99% = 10.56% with ZERO Simplu).
- The fixed-rate mortgage offers (3 or 5 years) switch to variable (IRCC + margin) after the fixed period.
- **Risk warning** (bank): variation of IRCC after the fixed period can raise the rate and the monthly instalment; borrowing in a currency different from income exposes to FX risk.
- Agent sensitivity hint (inferred): compute a stress case of +2 percentage points on the variable portion when showing mortgage affordability.
